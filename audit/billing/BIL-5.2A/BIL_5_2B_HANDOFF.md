# BIL-5.2B handoff

Before controlled operational actions, first complete the bounded source-owned in-doubt query contracts and the restricted-role multi-tenant HTTP/PostgreSQL proof. Any later action must remain in its owning module, require its existing step-up/authorization policy, and have an explicit audited operation. Do not add generic retry, replay, resubmission, posting, or quarantine-release controls to the aggregate workspace.
