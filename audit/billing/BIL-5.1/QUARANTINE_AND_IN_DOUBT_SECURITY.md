# Quarantine and in-doubt boundaries

The workspace calls no central Claim.MD inbox, account cursor, capture table, or platform recovery API. Unattributed central quarantine is therefore not listed, counted, searched, or previewed for tenant users.

M25 stage rows appear only through the tenant/provider-scoped review API. A held stage shows its source-backed hold reason; ambiguous matches and possible duplicates are indicated from the authorized stage summary. In-doubt submission records are not yet included because this workspace does not have an owner-projected M23 in-doubt list contract. No retry, replay, reassignment, quarantine release, posting, approval, or mutation control is available here.

An unavailable source is reported as unavailable/denied, not treated as an empty source. This is intentionally less revealing than an operator-wide quarantine view.
