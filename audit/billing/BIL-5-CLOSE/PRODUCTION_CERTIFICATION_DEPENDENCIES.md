# Production and certification dependencies

- Claim.MD account/environment certification and operational runbook approval.
- Payer/MAC certification for any future Medicare financial posting.
- Business authorization for CO write-offs, PR patient liability and PI treatment.
- Canonical A/R ownership and ledger policy.
- Production IdP/step-up certification for platform operations.
- Recovery execution contract with fencing, idempotency, rollback and financial-effect proof.

`CLAIMMD_ERA_CONTROLLED_POSTING_ENABLED=false` remains the production default.
