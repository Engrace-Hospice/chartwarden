# Client progress summary

WardenX now has a tenant-scoped Billing Operations workspace with source-owned M23, M24, M25 and M41 visibility, submission uncertainty worklists, administrative investigations, commercial payment reconciliation, and tenant-safe operational health.

Commercial ERA posting is controlled by immutable evidence, approval separation, duplicate protection and atomic M25 execution for the supported scenarios. Medicare financial posting, automatic posting, patient billing, unsupported adjustments and live recovery remain disabled.

Platform operators can inspect durable Claim.MD health and run a nonmutating recovery readiness assessment. Production activation still depends on external payer/vendor certification, business accounting policy and deployment-specific identity controls.
