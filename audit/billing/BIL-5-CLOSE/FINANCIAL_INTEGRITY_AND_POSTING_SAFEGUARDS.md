# Financial integrity and posting safeguards

The supported commercial posting path remains source-bound, approved, idempotent and default-disabled. Fully paid multi-claim and supported OA partial-payment behavior remain covered by prior private M25 suites and were not changed by closeout.

Medicare Claim.MD posting remains blocked. CO/PR/PI policy restrictions, PLB review, reversal/recoupment guards, duplicate financial-effect checks, and approver/executor separation remain intact. No A/R ledger was introduced; unsupported A/R remains explicitly unavailable.
