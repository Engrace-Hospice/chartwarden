# Remaining blockers

- The supported scope remains fully paid, no-adjustment, no-PLB, claim-level only. Partial payments, CAS, service-line allocation, reversals, recoupments, remarks/unknown content and cross-provider batches remain blocked.
- This is development functionality only. Claim.MD certification, external payer route certification and production authorization remain separate gates.
- PostgreSQL validation covers the private repository runner; it does not certify production operations or bank settlement.
