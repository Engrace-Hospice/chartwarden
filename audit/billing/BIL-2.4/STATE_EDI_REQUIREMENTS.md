# State EDI requirements evidence

Official pages were checked on 2026-11-08. Guides and enrollment requirements can change; implementation must pin and review the applicable guide/version before a route is enabled.

| State | Official evidence | What it establishes | What it does not establish for WardenX |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oregon | [OHA EDI resources](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/ohp/providers/pages/edi-resources.aspx), [OHA EDI enrollment](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/hsd/ohp/pages/edi.aspx) | OHA publishes an FFS institutional 837 guide; trading partner agreement, provider/submitter identity, and possible testing apply. | WardenX is not enrolled/tested and its JSON reference envelope is not the official 837I transport. No direct route can be inferred from a configured endpoint. |
| Washington | [HCA HIPAA EDI](https://www.hca.wa.gov/billers-providers-partners/prior-authorization-claims-and-billing/hipaa-electronic-data-interchange-edi) | ProviderOne publishes 837 Institutional and separate encounter guides; trading partner enrollment/TPA and transaction testing required. | No implemented ProviderOne transport or WardenX certification. Encounter guide does not mean provider payment claims can be routed through it. |
| Idaho | [Idaho provider enrollment](https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/providers/idaho-medicaid-providers/provider-enrollment) | Providers must register for an Idaho Medicaid Trading Partner Account and complete provider enrollment, agreement, W-9, and attachments. | A current hospice-specific EDI companion guide/route and WardenX transmission certification were not verified. |
| California | [DHCS provider information](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/medi-cal/Pages/for-Medi-Cal-Providers.aspx), [Medi-Cal managed care distinction](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/dataandstats/Pages/Managed-Care-Data-Quality-Monitoring-Frequently-Asked-Questions.aspx), [PACES encounter reporting](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/data-statistics/data-resources/medi-cal-managed-care-encounter-data-reporting/) | FFS enrollment/billing and contracting with individual MCPs are distinct. DHCS 837I managed-care encounter reporting is for MCP post-adjudication encounter reporting. | PACES encounter support is not a provider claim-payment route. Current FFS institutional guide details require DHCS source/document-center verification; no WardenX route is certified. |
| Nevada | [Nevada Medicaid EDI](https://www.medicaid.nv.gov/providers/edi) | Current page lists 837I FFS and separate 837I encounter guides; trading partner agreement, enrollment and testing/certification processes are described. | Guide availability does not mean WardenX has a configured, enrolled, tested, or authorized route. |

This is a source inventory, not a substitute for the current state manual, provider-specific contract, enrollment, test authorization, or trading-partner approval. No state-specific billing policy was guessed or implemented in this phase.
