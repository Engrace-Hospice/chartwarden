# Service-line matching

The outbound immutable evidence foundation is implemented for future supported Claim.MD M23/M41 attempts. The M25 matching service does not yet resolve an 835 service line through that evidence, a selected original attempt, and its 6R namespace. Therefore verified service-line matching remains **not implemented** in this phase. No automatic matching by code, amount, date, or position was introduced, and no line allocations are proposed.

Historical evidence unavailable and payer reference absent must remain explicit outcomes for the next matching integration.
