# Remaining blockers

This is not general partial-payment support. Patient-responsibility transfer, CO write-off, OA settlement, PI/other adjustments, multiple CAS entries, service-line adjudication, line allocation, PLB, reversals, recoupments, unknown segments, and mixed multi-claim batches containing a partial claim remain blocked.

The supported OA residual is preserved for review; an organization-level accounting policy is still required before assigning it to payer AR, patient AR, or write-off. Production and automatic Claim.MD posting remain disabled and are not authorized by development test success.
