# MODULE 08 — Encounters & Visit Notes (spec v0.2 "Locked")

## GRADE: **FAIL** — 3 BLOCKER / 10 MAJOR / 10 MINOR

The spec is, in many respects, the strongest clinical-documentation design in the suite: immutability-with-addenda (D2), the 24/48h cosignature billing-block (D3), the three-tier late-entry ladder (D4), the hard-block category list that *exceeds* §10.5.3 (D7), vocabulary version pinning (D11), and step-up signing (D13) are all excellent and survey-defensible. It nonetheless FAILS on three architecture/AI-governance invariants: it asserts the Anthropic BAA is signed when the governance documents say it is in negotiation, it builds against ten modules its manifest entry does not permit without proposing a manifest edit, and every one of its ten PHI tables omits the mandatory `provider_entity_id`/`location_id` tenancy columns.
