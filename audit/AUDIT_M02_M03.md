# MODULE 02 — IDENTITY & ORG

## Grade: **FAIL** (2 BLOCKER / 8 MAJOR / 9 MINOR)

The spec is unusually mature on session-class design, step-up mechanics, and HIPAA workforce lifecycle, but it claims and directly mutates Module 1-owned tables, and its notification delivery path violates the vendor-adapter mandate by design. Both would fail the §25.15 fitness pipeline as specced.

# Cross-Module Suspicions (for other auditors / cross-module pass)

1. **`packages/auth` reachability platform-wide:** Per the manifest, a module may import only its dependsOn codePaths + leaves — but `packages/auth` is not a leaf, and many modules whose routes MUST use `requirePermission`/`requireAbac` do not depend on 2/3 (e.g., M22 `[1,5,39]`, M29 `[5,11,40]`, M18 `[17]`, M35 `[34]`). M29's audit must check its dependency declaration, its registration of `external_interfaces.*` permission keys into the M3 catalog (mechanism per M03-F07), and SUD/Part 2 handling of the "unredact" surface.

Outbox subscription may be exempt from import boundaries, but the seam registry (ARCH-146) says dependsOn IS the cross-module contract list. Verify in those audits.
