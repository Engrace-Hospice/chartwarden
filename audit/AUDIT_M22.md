# AUDIT — Module 22 Document Management (WARDENX_MODULE_22_DOCUMENTS_SPEC_v0_4.md)

**Auditor protocol:** AUDITOR_INSTRUCTIONS.md v1.0; criteria packs CRITERIA_ARCHITECTURE.md, CRITERIA_SECURITY_AI.md, CRITERIA_OPS_DR_INTEGRATIONS.md; module-manifest.ts. Spec read in full (3,486 lines). BLOCKER findings verified directly against WARDENX_ARCHITECTURE_v0_11.md, WARDENX_SECURITY_HARDENING_v0_2.md, WARDENX_INTEGRATIONS_v0_1.md, WARDENX_SYSTEM_DESIGN_v0.3.md, boot.ts, module-manifest.ts.

## GRADE: **FAIL** — 3 BLOCKER / 16 MAJOR / 8 MINOR

The spec is unusually strong on its core AI-governance posture (never-auto-commit, per-field HIGH-risk approval, mandatory source-view confirmation, full provenance chain, M32-only AI invocation with deny-stubs, SUD detection bridge, Part 2 auto-tagging and eraser refusal). It fails on (1) a dependency proposal that creates manifest cycles and would fail the fitness pipeline by design, (2) PHI (patient last name) in signed-URL filenames, and (3) an uninventoried, un-BAA'd print/mail vendor integration carrying PHI.
