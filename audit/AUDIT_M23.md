## GRADE: **FAIL** — 6 BLOCKER / 9 MAJOR / 19 MINOR

This module produces Medicare claims; dimension I was applied at maximum severity. The spec is architecturally disciplined (outbox-only submission, seams, RLS, append-only audit surfaces, replacement chains, AI block) — but the **Medicare payment model it encodes is wrong in ways that would produce noncompliant claims and overbilling on day one**: the RHC rate tiering, the SIA mechanics, the wage-index basis, the claim wire-content model, and the late-NOE consequence are all incorrectly or incompletely encoded. Two of these errors originate in the architecture document itself (recorded as DOC-CONFLICTs below); the rest are module-level.
