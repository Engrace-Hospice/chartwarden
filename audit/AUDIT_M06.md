# AUDIT — Module 06: Admission, Election & Certification

**Spec audited:** `WARDENX_MODULE_06_ADMISSION_ELECTION_CERTIFICATION_SPEC_v0_4.md` (Locked v0.4 Consolidated, 2026-06-10, 4,017 lines — read in full)
**Auditor protocol:** `audit/_criteria/AUDITOR_INSTRUCTIONS.md` v1.0; criteria packs CRITERIA_ARCHITECTURE, CRITERIA_SECURITY_AI, CRITERIA_OPS_DR_INTEGRATIONS; `module-manifest.ts`
**Date:** 2026-06-10

---

## GRADE: **FAIL** — 6 BLOCKER / 17 MAJOR / 13 MINOR

This is the regulatory heart of the product and the spec is impressively engineered at the mechanical level (full DDL, GIST EXCLUDE no-overlap constraints, transactional outbox, narrow HL7 rails, override audit). But it misencodes or omits several core Medicare hospice election/certification rules (election statement addendum entirely absent; verbal-cert 2-day rule inverted; per-period election re-signing; late-F2F "override" path with no CMS basis), proposes a manifest edit that creates a dependency cycle, and places Drizzle repositories inside the pure domain package. Each of those alone fails the module.
