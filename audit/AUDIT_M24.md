## GRADE: **FAIL** — 2 BLOCKER / 11 MAJOR / 13 MINOR

The spec is exceptionally thorough on adapter architecture, idempotency, and AI-block discipline — and it correctly isolates state wire-format logic in `packages/medicaid-adapters/` per the §25 adapter mandate. It fails on (1) a dependency-graph rewrite that designs in module cycles and breaks named consumer modules, and (2) a Room & Board money-flow design that is internally contradictory and would produce incorrectly priced Medicaid claims by design. A cluster of Medicaid-domain omissions (patient liability/share-of-cost, TPL/crossover, retro-eligibility/timely filing) and several mis-encoded regulations (Cures Act scope, cap composition, state NOE-equivalents) add eleven MAJORs.
