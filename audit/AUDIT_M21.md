## GRADE: **FAIL** — 5 BLOCKER / 10 MAJOR / 6 MINOR

The spec is unusually strong on controlled-substance chain-of-custody design (dispatch/return/witness/5%-discrepancy/AI-prohibited zone) but fails on (1) the Medicare billing model it encodes for DME/supplies, (2) RLS conformance, (3) integration-catalog discipline for DME vendors and partner pharmacies, (4) a wholesale SQL-vs-Drizzle schema contradiction that makes the controlled-substance accounting layer ambiguous, and (5) dependency engineering against the authoritative manifest.
