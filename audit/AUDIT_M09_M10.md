# MODULE 09 — Clinical Assessments Suite

## Grade: **FAIL** (1 BLOCKER / 4 MAJOR / 7 MINOR)

The spec is one of the stronger clinical specs reviewed in structure and clinical content (14 scales, server-authoritative scoring, append-only corrections, license gating, floor-enforced thresholds), but it ships a CI-failing leaf-package violation by design, is built end-to-end on a dependency graph that contradicts the manifest while self-declaring "Locked," and misencodes 42 CFR Part 2 in a way that blocks mandated clinical documentation.

# MODULE 10 — HOPE Engine

## Grade: **FAIL** (2 BLOCKER / 9 MAJOR / 9 MINOR)

The timepoint engine concept is exactly what the architecture asks for (real-time, server-authoritative J2051, capture/submission tier split, append-only Correction RFA), and the iQIES adapter boundary matches INT-025/INT-044 precisely. But the spec (a) imports other modules' Drizzle schemas directly — a by-design fitness failure, (b) anchors the CMS 2-calendar-day SFV clock to the **signature** time (with a grace allowance) instead of the HOPE **visit** date — an incorrect encoding of the single most consequential HOPE rule, and (c) contradicts the authoritative System Design and retention matrix on partitioning and retention, contradicts SD-059 on queue topology, omits the iQIES **inactivation** flow and any instrument-version pin, and is internally inconsistent about which HOPE sections even exist.
