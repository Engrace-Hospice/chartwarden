# MODULE 14 — IDG (Interdisciplinary Group)

## Grade: **FAIL** (2 BLOCKER / 8 MAJOR / 9 MINOR)

The spec is unusually complete on lifecycle mechanics, quorum-at-decision-time enforcement, survey-artifact immutability (frozen packets, final minutes + §164.526-pattern amendments), and the canonical `idg.decision_recorded` contract. It fails on (1) the CoP 418.56(d) 15-day clock being enforceable only at the recurring-policy level with unguarded cancel/reschedule escape paths and no missed-review escalation, and (2) a manifest/boundary state that fails its own `arch:fitness` gate (M32/M26 consumption omitted from the proposed dependsOn patch).

# MODULE 16 — Bereavement

## Grade: **FAIL** (1 BLOCKER / 7 MAJOR / 8 MINOR)

The regulatory core is strong: death-date-anchored 13-month program with platform-floor/tenant-tighten-only cadence (correct reading of 418.64(d)'s "up to 1 year" as a floor the program exceeds), survivors modeled as first-class **non-patient** records with their own consent/opt-out/risk/schedule, correct refusal to transfer the decedent's consents to survivors (Part 2 / HHS guidance), §164.502 50-year decedent awareness, personal-representative authority isolated in M39, and a verified M6→M16 death-event contract (`m6.bereavement.eligible` + external-source cosign gating).
