# MODULE 07 — CARE PLAN (IPOC)

## GRADE: **FAIL** (1 BLOCKER / 6 MAJOR / 8 MINOR)

The spec is structurally strong — the hybrid versioning model (D1), patient-stated-goal verbatim preservation (D6), SQL-level SUD redaction (D7), and the 5-day/15-day gate machinery are genuinely survey-grade design. It FAILS on one regulatory mis-encoding (NP in the IDG physician seat) and carries material gaps in CoP 418.56 content completeness, the review-clock reset semantics, and manifest conformance.
