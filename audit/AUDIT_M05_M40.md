## Grade: **FAIL** (1 BLOCKER / 8 MAJOR / 10 MINOR)

The patient spine is well designed in most clinical respects — the match pipeline, merge reversibility, §164.526 workflow, SSN envelope encryption, 42 CFR Part 2 snapshot pattern, and the CaseloadProvider seam are all strong. ## Cross-Module Suspicions (M05)

1. **`patient_address.geocode_lat/lng` shared-write risk** — three conflicting writers named (M5 job, M17 "on accept", routing-client). Whoever wins, if M17 writes these columns it is a cross-module table write; M17's auditor must check (batch emphasis: shared-write tables).
2. **Attending physician FK** — M40 expects `patient.attending_physician_id`; M5 has none (F08). M6 (CTI/F2F attending), M23 (§11.A attending-NPI rule), and M28 (Practitioner reference) auditors should verify what they join on.
3. **M29 outbox payload carries full PID + SSN** (F18) — M29 auditor: PHI minimization/encryption of `hl7_inbound_proposal.accepted` payloads, `parsed_segments_cipher` handling, and outbox_event exposure.
4. **M3 boot-gate scope creep** — M5 requires M3's `validateSeams` to demand `PatientReader`/`PatientMutator` (and M40 adds `BaaStatusProvider`/`MdmEntityProvider`); ARCH-234 hosts `validateSeams` in M1. M1/M3 auditors should confirm a single owner and the required-seam list.
5. **M17 CaseloadProvider contribution** — ARCH-234 says Modules 5/17 implement the seam; M5 implements the union and consumes M17's `shift.visit_assigned` Valkey cache. M17's auditor must verify it publishes that cache and does NOT register a second CaseloadProvider.
6. **M22/M28 redaction dependency** — M5's Part 2 enforcement delegates outbound redaction to M22/M28 reading the snapshot; those audits must verify the consult-before-disclose hook exists.
