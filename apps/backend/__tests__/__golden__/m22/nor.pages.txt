Notice of patient rights
[42 CFR 418.52(a)]
I have received, and the hospice has explained to me, a written notice of my rights as a hospice patient. The notice
explains my rights, including the right to be informed of my rights before care is furnished; to be treated with dignity and
respect; to have my person and property respected; to take part in planning my care; to choose my attending physician;
to refuse care or treatment; to voice grievances without discrimination or reprisal; and to have my records kept
confidential. The hospice has also given me information about its policies on advance directives.
