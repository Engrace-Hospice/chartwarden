# Phase 3 — claim assembly completeness: PR notes

Paste-ready notes for the PR body. Branch `feat/claim-assembly-hospice-lines`; M23 spec v0.3.2.

## What this changes

- **Visit lines.** Every billable M8 visit is its own 15-minute-unit line; CHC is one dated line per day; the claim total
  is the sum of its line charges (`claim_total_matches_lines`). Visit charges are tenant-entered, effective-dated and
  append-only per discipline (`billing.visit_charge.read/admin`); a missing charge fails assembly closed.
  A visit with no documented duration is skipped and surfaced by the warn rule `visit_lines_reportable`.
- **Therapy disciplines.** M8 gains `pt` / `ot` / `slp` and `pt_visit` / `ot_visit` / `slp_visit`.
- **Admission type and point of origin.** M6 owns UB-04 FL 14 / FL 15 (nullable, never backfilled); snapshotted to the claim,
  refreshed on every validation; blocking rule `admission_codes_present`; the 837I document carries both.
- **Value codes 61 / G8.** Resolved from the patient's / facility's ZIP against CMS hospice wage-index geography
  (ZIP → county → CBSA / rural 999NN / transition 5000N). Blocking rule `cbsa_value_codes_present`.
  Connecticut resolves through its 2022 planning regions; a PO-box ZIP is placed by its ZIP3 only when every ZIP sharing the
  prefix agrees; anything else is unresolved and blocks (nothing defaults to rural).
- **Pricing.** Home levels of care use the beneficiary's residence-area wage index; GIP / inpatient respite use the facility's.
  Each day of a claim crossing Oct 1 is priced with its own fiscal year's rate table and wage index. The claim keeps one set of
  end-of-period CBSA codes (as the Pricer does: BENE-CBSA from value code 61, PROV-CBSA from G8) and takes each fiscal year's
  published index for those codes, so a patient who moves mid-claim does not change the codes.
- **G8 facility.** The LATEST facility that served the inpatient (0655/0656) level-of-care periods (CMS Pub. 100-04 ch. 11
  §30.3). Taken from M37 `ipu_admission` (keyed by the M6 level-of-care period; owned unit or arranged facility) so a nursing-home
  resident's GIP stay at a hospital is priced at the hospital; M5 facility residence spans stand in only for stays M37 does not know.
  A tie on the last inpatient day between different (or missing) ZIPs, or no facility at all, blocks.
- **Tooling.** `bun run db:seed:wage-index -- --ccn <CCN> --zip <ZIP>` derives `tenant_wage_index` from the geography.
  Demo fixture: CCN 381588 = Engrace Hospice, Pendleton OR, ZIP 97801 (confirmed against CMS Provider Data).
  There is no production default.

## Legacy and draft claims (accepted)

Draft claims assembled before this change carry no admission codes, value codes or visit lines, and their total may include
SIA. They fail `claim_total_matches_lines`, `admission_codes_present` and `cbsa_value_codes_present` until they are
re-assembled or the admission codes are recorded. **Accepted: production is demo data, so there is no remediation script;
the demo seed produces records that pass the new rules.**

## Open assumptions

- A ZCTA approximates a USPS ZIP; the ZIP3 fallback is exact only where the prefix is unanimous.
- The facility address key is `facility.primary_address.postalCode` (the seed and repo convention).
- The ch. 11 §30.3 rules (value codes 61 / G8: end-of-period residence, latest facility; Pricer BENE-CBSA / PROV-CBSA) were verified
  against the manual text (clm104c11.pdf). 42 CFR 418.306(c) was verified too, but it only defines the labor market by OMB CBSAs, the
  wage adjustment and the 5% cap; the SSA §1814(i)(2)(D) citation was dropped because it could not be verified here.
- **SIA across Oct 1.** The SIA add-on is priced with the fiscal year of the death date: that year's CHC hourly rate (rate-table
  version effective on the death date, or the claim's first day when the death is in the claim's first year) and the
  continuous-home-care wage index (that year's residence-area index where the claim is priced by area, else the provider index).
  A death in a fiscal year the claim does not touch (a claim ending Sep 30, a death on Oct 1) is priced from inputs read on demand;
  if that year's rate table or index is unavailable the claim fails closed with a typed 422 - there is no fallback. The factor is
  resolved only when an SIA is payable (qualifying visits exist), so an all-inpatient deceased claim with no SIA never needs a
  residence index (assembly and revalidation share the same function).
  **What is and is not recorded:** each `hipps_calculation` segment records the fiscal year and wage-index factor it was priced
  with (jsonb, no migration) - that covers the per-diem math. The SIA's own basis is **not** persisted: `sia_calculation` stores no
  CHC rate, fiscal year or factor, and the rate-table version chosen by date is not recorded anywhere. The SIA factor can be
  recovered from the segments only when the claim has a home (RHC/CHC) segment in the death's fiscal year (its recorded factor is
  the CHC factor); it cannot when the claim has none in that year (an all-inpatient claim, or a death in a year the claim does not
  touch). The original CHC rate cannot be re-derived at all if the rate table was superseded since. Closing this needs columns on
  `sia_calculation` (rate, fiscal year, factor, rate-table id) - deliberately not done here.
- Deferred by decision: PM modifier, Q5006 inpatient-facility summary reporting, diagnoses (Phase 4). No web screen yet for the
  visit-charge schedule (API only).
