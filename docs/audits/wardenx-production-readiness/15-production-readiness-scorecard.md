## Weighted overall

Unweighted mean ≈ **2.4 / 5**. Weighting the safety-critical categories (security, DB integrity, tenant isolation, workflow, reliability, deployment) more heavily pulls the effective posture **below 2.4**, consistent with the **Not Production Ready** verdict.

## How the score moves

- Closing Phases 0–2 (containment + wiring + security/RLS) lifts Architecture, Security, DB integrity, Reliability, and Observability into the 3–4 band and is the precondition for any pilot.
- Closing Phase 4 (workflows) lifts Workflow/UI completeness from ~1 to 3+.
- Phase 8 (ops) is what moves Deployment/Observability off the floor; it is last because it is only meaningful on a wired, secured, operable system.
