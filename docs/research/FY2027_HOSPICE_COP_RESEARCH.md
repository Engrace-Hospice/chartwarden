## 1. 42 CFR 418.24: election statement and addendum (in force from 2026-10-01)

FR-F amended **(b)(6), the (c) introductory text, (c)(9), (c)(10) and (d)**. Paragraphs (c)(1)–(8)
were not amended, but the revised (c) introductory text now applies them to every election
on or after 2026-10-01. The text below is from eCFR "current" and matches the FR-F amendatory
text word for word.

### 1a. § 418.24(b): content of the election statement (verbatim)

> (b) *Content of election statement.* The election statement must include the following:
> (1) Identification of the particular hospice and of the attending physician that will provide care to the individual. The individual or representative must acknowledge that the identified attending physician was his or her choice.
> (2) The individual's or representative's acknowledgement that he or she has been given a full understanding of the palliative rather than curative nature of hospice care, as it relates to the individual's terminal illness and related conditions.
> (3) Acknowledgement that the individual has been provided information on the hospice's coverage responsibility and that certain Medicare services, as set forth in paragraph (g) of this section, are waived by the election. For Hospice elections beginning on or after October 1, 2020, this would include providing the individual with information indicating that services unrelated to the terminal illness and related conditions are exceptional and unusual and hospice should be providing virtually all care needed by the individual who has elected hospice.
> (4) The effective date of the election, which may be the first day of hospice care or a later date, but may be no earlier than the date of the election statement.
> (5) For Hospice elections beginning on or after October 1, 2020, the Hospice must provide information on individual cost-sharing for hospice services.
> (6) For Hospice elections beginning on or after October 1, 2026, the hospice must provide the individual (or representative) an election statement addendum, as set forth in paragraphs (c) and (d) of this section, which includes any conditions, items, services, and drugs the hospice has determined to be unrelated to the individual's terminal illness and related conditions and would not be covered by the hospice.
> (7) For Hospice elections beginning on or after October 1, 2020, the Hospice must provide information on the Beneficiary and Family Centered Care Quality Improvement Organization (BFCC-QIO), including the right to immediate advocacy and BFCC-QIO contact information.
> (8) The signature of the individual or representative.

### 1b. § 418.24(c): content of the addendum (verbatim, every element)

> (c) *Content of hospice election statement addendum.* For hospice elections beginning on or after October 1, 2026, the hospice must provide the individual (or representative) an election statement addendum. The election statement addendum (and its updates) must include the following:
> (1) The addendum must be titled "Patient Notification of Hospice Non-Covered Items, Services, and Drugs."
> (2) Name of the hospice.
> (3) Individual's name and hospice medical record identifier.
> (4) Identification of the individual's terminal illness and related conditions.
> (5) A list of the individual's conditions present on hospice admission (or upon plan of care update) and the associated items, services, and drugs not covered by the hospice because they have been determined by the hospice to be unrelated to the terminal illness and related conditions.
> (6) A written clinical explanation, in language the individual (or representative) can understand, as to why the identified conditions, items, services, and drugs are considered unrelated to the individual's terminal illness and related conditions and not needed for pain or symptom management. This clinical explanation must be accompanied by a general statement that the decision as to whether or not conditions, items, services, and drugs are related is made for each patient and that the individual should share this clinical explanation with other health care providers from which they seek items, services, or drugs unrelated to their terminal illness and related conditions.
> (7) References to any relevant clinical practice, policy, or coverage guidelines.
> (8) Information on the following:
> (i) *Purpose of Addendum.* The purpose of the addendum is to notify the individual (or representative), in writing, of those conditions, items, services, and drugs the hospice will not be covering because the hospice has determined they are unrelated to the individual's terminal illness and related conditions.
> (ii) *Right to Immediate Advocacy.* The addendum must include language that immediate advocacy is available through the Medicare Beneficiary and Family Centered Care-Quality Improvement Organization (BFCC-QIO) if the individual (or representative) disagrees with the hospice's determination.
> (9) Name and signature of the individual (or representative) and date signed, along with a statement that signing this addendum (and its updates) is only acknowledgement of receipt of the addendum and not the individual's (or representative's) agreement with the hospice's determinations. If the individual (or representative) refuses to sign the addendum, the hospice must document on the addendum the reason the addendum was not signed and the addendum would become part of the patient's medical record. The addendum must also be available for non-hospice providers and Medicare contractors, although non-hospice providers and Medicare contractors are not required to sign the addendum.
> (10) Date the hospice furnished the addendum to the individual (or representative).

### 1c. § 418.24(d): timeframes (verbatim)

> (d) *Timeframes for the hospice election statement addendum.* (1) For hospice elections beginning on or after October 1, 2026, the hospice must provide the individual (or representative) an election statement addendum, in writing, as set forth in paragraph (c) of this section, at the time of the hospice election (that is, within the first 5 days of the effective date of the hospice election). The hospice must also file this information with the election statement, as set forth in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section, to be available for the individual (or representative), non-hospice providers, and Medicare contractors.
> (2) If there are any changes to the plan of care during the course of hospice care that impact the addendum determinations, the hospice must update the addendum, within 3 days, with the contents described in paragraph (c) of this section, and provide these updates, in writing, to the individual (or representative), as well as update the addendum on file in order to communicate these changes to the individual (or representative), non-hospice providers, and Medicare contractors.
> (3) If the individual dies, revokes, or is discharged within the required timeframe for providing the addendum (and its updates) (as outlined in paragraphs (d)(1) and (2) of this section), and before the hospice has provided the addendum (and its updates), the addendum would not be required to be provided, in writing, to the individual (or representative). The hospice must note the reason the addendum (and its updates) was not completed and/or provided, in writing, to the individual (or representative) and this note would become part of the patient's medical record. If completed, the hospice must still file the addendum (and its updates) with the election statement, as set forth in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section, to be available for the individual (or representative), non-hospice providers, and Medicare contractors.
> (4) If the individual dies, revokes, or is discharged prior to signing the addendum (or its updates) (as outlined in paragraphs (d)(1) and (2) with the required contents described in paragraph (c) of this section), the addendum would not be required to be signed in order for the hospice to receive payment. The hospice must note (on the addendum itself) the reason the addendum (and any updates) was not signed and the addendum would become part of the patient's medical record.

### 1d. Implementation facts drawn from the sources

- **Scope:** every Medicare hospice election with an election that begins on or after **2026-10-01**. It no longer depends on a request. FR-F's final decision reads: "we are finalizing as proposed our proposal to make the election statement addendum mandatory for all hospice elections, not just upon request."
- **What triggers an update:** "any changes to the plan of care during the course of hospice care that impact the addendum determinations" (§ 418.24(d)(2)). There is a **3-day** window to update the addendum, give it in writing, and update the copy on file. The regulation does not list specific triggers. Under the CoP at § 418.56, plan-of-care updates come from IDG review.
- **Day counting:** the regulation says "5 days" and "3 days". The MLN item (2026-10-01) says "**5 calendar days** of the effective date of the hospice election" and "within **3 calendar days** of the plan of care change".
- **Refusal to sign:** document the reason **on the addendum**, and it becomes part of the medical record (§ 418.24(c)(9)).
- **Death, revocation or discharge** before the addendum is given (within the window): no written delivery is required. Note the reason in the medical record. If the addendum was completed, it must still be filed with the election statement (§ 418.24(d)(3)).
- **Death, revocation or discharge before signature:** no signature is needed for payment. Note the reason **on the addendum itself** (§ 418.24(d)(4)).
- **No non-covered items:** an addendum is still required, and it records that determination. FR-F: "An addendum documenting that all items, services, and drugs have been determined to be related to the terminal illness and related conditions is not a blank form". FR-F also says "there is no standardized method to document that all services are covered by the hospice".
- **Format and signature:** there is no required standardized form. A written copy must be provided. An electronic copy is allowed only as a supplement. "electronic or written signatures are acceptable; however, a hospice cannot require an electronic signature in lieu of a written signature if the beneficiary or representative does not have access to the necessary resources or the understanding required" (FR-F). The MLN item reads "Must be furnished in writing (paper or electronic)". Those two statements are in some tension, so **the safe implementation is to always produce a printable written copy.**
- **Condition for payment** (MLN): "Condition for payment is satisfied when there's a valid signed addendum in the beneficiary's medical record with the hospice". That is in addition to the documented-reason exceptions above.
- **Title used by CMS:** "Patient Notification of Hospice Non-Covered Items, Services, and Drugs" (§ 418.24(c)(1)).
- **CMS model addendum (Aug 2026):** "Model Example of 'Patient Notification of Hospice Non-Covered Items, Services, and Drugs' — Updated August 2026" (MODEL). Its fields are:
  - Patient Name
  - Patient MRN
  - Hospice Agency Name
  - Date Furnished
  - "Diagnoses Unrelated to Terminal Illness and Related Conditions" (slots 1–8)
  - "Diagnoses Related to Terminal Illness and Related Conditions" (slots 1–8)
  - Table "Items/Services/Drugs | Reason for Non-coverage"
  - Purpose paragraph
  - Note that the hospice decides relatedness for each patient and that the patient should share it with other providers
  - Right to Immediate Advocacy (BFCC-QIO), with the link https://www.cms.gov/medicare/quality/quality-improvement-organizations/family-centered-care, 1-800-MEDICARE, and TTY 1-877-486-2048
  - The receipt-only acknowledgement statement
  - Signature of Beneficiary, Signature of Beneficiary Representative, Date Signed

  The model also defines "Date Furnished" in its own words: "The 'date furnished' is defined as when the beneficiary (or representative) receives an addendum (or its updates), in writing, within 5 days of the effective date of election (or within 3 days of any updates made to the plan of care that impact the addendum determinations), not the date of the signature."
- **The model election statement was also updated in Aug 2026.** It now has a section titled "Your Addendum: 'Patient Notification of Hospice Non-Covered Items, Services, and Drugs'" that tells the patient about the 5-day and 3-day obligations. It also has BFCC-QIO name and phone/website fields. LeadingAge (secondary source) reported that CMS posted these models on 2026-09-11. The PDFs themselves say "August 2026".
- **Enforcement discretion** (MLN Connects 2026-10-01, verbatim): "To help hospices adjust to the new requirement, from October 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026, while hospices are required to furnish the addendum to every electing beneficiary, CMS will exercise enforcement discretion during this three-month window. During this time, Medicare Administrative Contractors will not deny claims solely because the addendum is missing or incomplete and will focus on educating hospices to help them come into full compliance." Neither the regulation text nor FR-F contains this discretion. The requirement itself is fully effective 2026-10-01.
- **Possible stale cross-reference in eCFR (not changed by FR-F):** § 418.26(c)(2) still says "benefits waived under § 418.24(e)", but the waiver is now § 418.24(g).

## 2. § 418.26(b): discharge order (verbatim, effective 2026-10-01)

> (b) *Discharge order.* Prior to discharging a patient for any reason listed in paragraph (a) of this section, the hospice must obtain a written physician's discharge order from the hospice medical director (or physician designee, as defined at § 418.3) or physician member of the interdisciplinary group. If a patient has an attending physician involved in his or her care, this physician should be consulted before discharge and his or her review and decision included in the discharge note.

- **Who may sign:**
  - the hospice medical director,
  - the physician designee (§ 418.3), or
  - the physician member of the IDG.

  The attending physician is consulted but is not a signer. PAs are **not** included (FR-F declined that request).
- **Effective date:** 2026-10-01. The previous text, per eCFR as of 2026-09-30, named only "the hospice medical director". FR-F calls this a conforming change ("Final Decision: We are finalizing the proposed additions to § 418.26(b) as proposed.").

## 3. § 418.22(a)(4)(ii) and (b)(4): telehealth F2F and attestation

### (a)(4)(ii): verbatim, as revised by FR-F, effective 2026-10-01

> (ii) During a Public Health Emergency, as defined in §400.200 of this chapter, or through December 31, 2027, whichever is later, if the face-to-face encounter conducted by a hospice physician or hospice nurse practitioner is for the sole purpose of hospice recertification, such encounter may occur via telecommunications technology and is considered an administrative expense. Telecommunications technology means the use of interactive multimedia communications equipment that includes, at a minimum, the use of audio and video equipment permitting two-way, real-time interactive communication between the patient and the distant site hospice physician or hospice nurse practitioner. For face-to-face encounters occurring on or after January 1, 2027, hospices must report any such encounters occurring via telecommunications technology on the claim, in accordance with guidance issued by CMS. Beginning January 31, 2026, telehealth may not be used for the face-to-face recertification encounter if any of the following conditions apply:
> (A) The hospice patient is located in an area subject to a hospice enrollment moratorium under section 1866(j)(7) of the Act;
> (B) The patient is receiving care from a hospice provider that is subject to enhanced oversight pursuant to section 1866(j)(3) of the Act; or
> (C) The face-to-face encounter is conducted by a hospice physician or nurse practitioner who is not enrolled in Medicare under section 1866(j) and is not an opt-out physician or practitioner (as defined in section 1802(b)(6)(D) of the Act.

Key dates:

- **Regulation text:** effective 2026-10-01.
- **Telehealth allowed:** through 2027-12-31.
- **Exclusions (A)–(C):** apply to encounters from 2026-01-31. That date comes from CAA 2026 § 6209(f)(1)(B), Pub. L. 119-75, 2026-02-03.
- **Claim reporting:** required for encounters from 2027-01-01 (CAA 2026 § 6209(f)(2)).
- **In-person F2F encounters are not reported on claims.** FR-F: "We will not require that in-person face-to-face encounters for the purposes of recertification to be collected on claims."
- **The prior regulation text lagged the statute.** eCFR as of 2026-09-30 still read "through September 30, 2025", while CAA 2026 had already extended the allowance in statute.

### (b)(4): verbatim (current; this was NOT changed by the FY 2027 rule)

> (4) The physician or nurse practitioner who performs the face-to-face encounter with the patient described in paragraph (a)(4) of this section must attest in writing that he or she had a face-to-face encounter with the patient, including the date of that visit. The attestation must include the physician's or nurse practitioner's signature and the date it was signed. The attestation could be a separate and distinct section of, or an addendum to, the recertification or a clinical note that indicates the face-to-face encounter occurred, and includes the clinical findings of the face-to-face encounter, the date of the visit, the signature of the physician or nurse practitioner who conducted the face-to-face encounter, and the date of the signature. If the attestation of the nurse practitioner or a non-certifying hospice physician is a separate and distinct section of, or an addendum to, the recertification, the attestation shall state that the clinical findings of that visit were provided to the certifying physician for use in determining continued eligibility for hospice care.

- **Effective date: 2025-10-01**, from the **FY 2026** final rule (90 FR 37416; eCFR amendment citation 90 FR 37430, Aug. 5, 2025). eCFR version history shows a § 418.22 version dated 2025-10-01. The text as of 2025-09-30 did not include the signature/date or clinical-note language.
- **Effect:** a clinical note can serve as the attestation if it contains the encounter's clinical findings, the visit date, and the signature and signature date of the physician or NP who conducted it. In that case, the "clinical findings were provided to the certifying physician" statement is required only for the separate-section and addendum forms.
- **Related, unchanged text:** § 418.22(b)(3)(v) requires the 3rd-and-later recertification narrative to explain why the F2F clinical findings support a prognosis of 6 months or less. § 418.22(a)(4)(i) limits F2F to the 3rd benefit period recertification and every one after it, within 30 calendar days before the period starts.

## 4. HCPCS G0679 (CR 14495)

- **CR / transmittals:**
  - CR 14495, Pub. 100-04, Medicare Claims Processing Manual, ch. 11 § 30.3 (revised).
  - Transmittal **13860** (2026-07-10) was **rescinded and replaced by Transmittal 13944** (2026-09-02) "to delete business requirement 14495.4. All other information remains the same."
  - MLN Matters **MM14495** (revised 2026-09-02).
- **Effective / implementation:** effective **2027-01-01**, "the date of service"; implementation 2027-01-04.
- **Descriptor (verbatim):** "G0679: Hospice face-to-face encounter conducted by a hospice physician or hospice nurse practitioner for recertification via telecommunications technology (this telehealth encounter is considered an administrative expense)."
- **Manual text (§ 30.3, "Telehealth Service Reporting", verbatim):** "Effective January 1, 2027, Hospices shall report face-to-face encounters conducted by a hospice physician or hospice nurse practitioner for recertification via telecommunications technology on hospice claims. Hospices shall submit the use of telecommunications technology when conducting face-to-face encounters for recertification, on the hospice claim via G-0679. ... Hospices shall report the encounter via telecommunications technology in line-item detail, and each service must be reported as a separately dated line under the appropriate revenue code for each discipline furnishing the service."
- **Business requirements:**
  - 14495.1: MACs "shall allow ... G0679 on claims with dates of service on or after January 1, 2027."
  - 14495.2: return to provider (RTP) "if the Type of Bill (TOB) is other than 81x or 82x."
  - 14495.3: RTP "if reported with revenues codes other than 0657."
  - 14495.4: "This business requirement has been deleted."
- **Claims and bill types:** hospice claims, TOB **81X** (freestanding) or **82X** (hospital-based).
- **Revenue code:** **0657** only.
- **Line item date:** a separately dated line, so the line-item date of service is the date of the telehealth F2F encounter. That date is inferred from "separately dated line"; the CR does not literally say "date of the encounter".
- **Units: UNCONFIRMED.** Neither the CR nor MM14495 specifies units. One unit per encounter is a reasonable assumption, but no primary source states it.
- **Charges: UNCONFIRMED.** Not specified. The descriptor calls the encounter an administrative expense, so it is not separately paid. Whether a $0.00 or nominal charge is expected is not stated. Ask the MAC, or check the MAC's January 2027 guidance.
- **Benefit-period scope:** the code reports recertification F2F encounters only, which § 418.22(a)(4)(i) limits to the 3rd benefit period and later. **No CR business requirement adds a benefit-period edit.** Whether MACs edit G0679 against the benefit period is **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Deleted BR 14495.4:** its original wording is **UNCONFIRMED**. The R13860 PDF on cms.gov now contains only the rescission notice. A secondary source (hccbuddy.com) says it was an RTP when only revenue code 0657 appears with no other services, but this could not be verified from a primary source.
- **Not in the CR:** practitioner NPI/enrollment checks. FR-F says "the use of the G-code is not intended to be used to check provider identification or enrollment status."
- **NP physician services on revenue code 0657 normally require modifier GV** (manual note). The G0679 instruction does not mention GV, so applying GV to G0679 is **UNCONFIRMED / not required by CR 14495.**

## 5. Admission recommendation by the IDG physician member

- The section that changed is **§ 418.25 (Admission to hospice care), paragraphs (a) and (b)**. It is a payment-subpart (subpart B) regulation, not a CoP.
- **Final rule:** FY 2026 Hospice final rule, 90 FR 37416 (amendment citation 90 FR 37431, Aug. 5, 2025). **Effective 2025-10-01**, confirmed by eCFR versions: the 2025-09-30 text names only the "medical director (or the physician designee...)".
- **Not changed by the FY 2027 rule.** FR-F only recounts it as background to the § 418.26(b) conforming change.
- **Verbatim, current:**

> (a) The hospice admits a patient only on the recommendation of the medical director (or the physician designee, as defined in § 418.3) or the physician member of the hospice interdisciplinary group, in consultation with, or with input from, the patient's attending physician (if any).
> (b) In reaching a decision to certify that the patient is terminally ill, the hospice medical director (or the physician designee, as defined in § 418.3) or the physician member of the hospice interdisciplinary group, must consider at least the following information:
> (1) Diagnosis of the terminal condition of the patient.
> (2) Other health conditions, whether related or unrelated to the terminal condition.
> (3) Current clinically relevant information supporting all diagnoses.

- **Related CoP: § 418.102(b)/(c).** Medical director, or physician designee if the medical director is unavailable, or physician member of the IDG, for initial certification and recertification review. These came from the FY 2025 final rule (89 FR 64272, Aug. 6, 2024), effective 2024-10-01. **The admission decision itself is in § 418.25, not § 418.102.**
- **Same signer set across 418.22(c)(1)(i), 418.25, 418.26(b) and 418.102(b)/(c):**
  - medical director,
  - physician designee,
  - physician member of the IDG.

## 6. FY 2027 payment: update, cap, HQRP penalty, rates

- **Payment update:** **2.3%** = 3.2% (2023-based IPPS market basket) minus 0.9 pp productivity (FR-F). The proposed rule had 2.4%. **The 2026-09-30 correction (FR-C) did NOT change the 2.3% update.** It fixed a wage-index error: hospital CCN 250078 had been wrongly excluded as a rural emergency hospital (REH). That fix changed the wage-index standardization factors, so it changed the national rates.
- **Cap:** **$36,174.75**, which is the FY 2026 cap of $35,361.44 × 1.023. FR-C did not correct it. § 418.309 was amended: "2033" became "2035" (CAA 2026 § 6218).
- **HQRP non-compliance:** a reduction of **4 percentage points**, giving a 2.3 − 4 = **−1.7%** update (factor 0.983).

## 7. SSVI: Service and Spending Variation Index

- **What it is:**
  - A CMS-calculated, claims-only, descriptive scoring tool. Hospices submit nothing.
  - It uses **nine claims-based metrics**: 8 utilization metrics worth 0 or 1 point each, plus total non-hospice spending worth 0–8 points by octile. That gives a total of **0–16**.
  - A higher score signals more potential concern. FR-F: "We would consider a hospice with 13 or more points an outlier as it would be at 99th percentile".
  - It is used for program-integrity targeting (medical review, education, investigations) and for public transparency.
- **Table 9 metrics (FR-F, verbatim names), threshold, and points:**

| # | Metric | Threshold (FY 2025 value) | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Providing no Continuous Home Care and no General Inpatient Care | = 0 (no CHC and no GIP) | 1 |
| 2 | Percentage of RHC days provided in a nursing home or skilled nursing facility | ≥ 40% | 1 |
| 3 | Percent of the last two RHC days of life with visits | ≤ 25th pct (85.7%) | 1 |
| 4 | Percentage of total discharges that are live discharges | ≥ 75th pct (47.5%) | 1 |
| 5 | Percentage of discharges with a length of stay over 180 days | ≥ 75th pct (33.2%) | 1 |
| 6 | Average skilled nursing minutes on RHC | ≤ 25th pct (9.8 min/day) | 1 |
| 7 | Weekend RHC days with a skilled visit (nursing, MSW, or therapy) as % of total RHC days | ≤ 25th pct (4.8%) | 1 |
| 8 | Percentage of live discharges where beneficiaries return to the same hospice in seven days | ≥ 75th pct (15%) | 1 |
| 9 | Total non-hospice spending (octiles of hospices) | >0 and ≤ $6,352.84 → 1; ≤ $20,612.10 → 2; ≤ $42,911.79 → 3; ≤ $76,801.05 → 4; ≤ $133,440.80 → 5; ≤ $246,123.10 → 6; ≤ $517,204.40 → 7; > $517,204.40 → 8 (each band's lower bound is exclusive) | 0–8 |

  - The rule describes metric 3 as "Percent of Beneficiaries who Died in Hospice and Last Two Days were RHC with at Least One Skilled Visit During Last Two Days".
  - Total non-hospice spending of $0 implies 0 points. That is inferred from ">0" being the lowest band.
  - Non-hospice spending counts only Medicare-paid Part A/B/D amounts on hospice election days, excluding admission and live-discharge days (FR-F Table 5/7/8 notes).
- **Percentile thresholds are recomputed every FY.** "We plan to determine the SSVI for individual hospices each FY using that applicable year's data". The FY 2025 values above are examples, not fixed constants.
- **Data period:**
  - FY 2024 claims: 6,735 hospices (pulled from the CCW VRDC 2025-05-09).
  - FY 2025 claims: 6,673 hospices (pulled 2026-05-12).
  - Future years will be published with each annual final rule.
- **Public reporting:**
  - CMS posts the metrics, scores, and an "SSVI Overview" methodology document in the Downloads of the hospice rule page (CMS-1851-F) and on the Hospice Information Center (https://www.cms.gov/hospice-information-center, announcement at https://www.cms.gov/hospice-news-announcements#SSVI).
  - It was first posted with the FY 2027 **proposed** rule (CMS-1851-P), and FR-F says it will be updated with each final rule.
  - The exact posting date of the final-rule SSVI files is **UNCONFIRMED**. The announcement page gives no date.
- **Relationship to the Hospice Care Index (HCI): it does NOT replace the HCI.** FR-F: "The SSVI is designed to complement the HCI's claim-based measures ... the measures are claims-based and will not replace any existing measures".
- **HQRP / Care Compare:**
  - The SSVI is **not** an HQRP measure, and it is not finalized for Care Compare.
  - It is presented in the payment/program-integrity section (III.B.2), not in the HQRP section.
  - It is published on CMS hospice web pages, not on Medicare.gov Care Compare.
  - There is no hospice submission, no preview report, and no reconsideration process. FR-F: "it is unclear how we would include a reconsideration process ... or a provider-specific preview".
  - Separately, the HQRP change in FR-F adds a Care Compare **icon for hospices that fail HQRP reporting**: no earlier than FY 2028 (2027-10-01), based on CY 2026 submissions, under the 90%-within-30-days HOPE threshold. That is unrelated to the SSVI.
- **Final decision (FR-F):** "we are maintaining the design of the SSVI using the current measures and scoring assignments."
