## Context

Checked in the code, not assumed:

- **M19 already owns the after-hours clinical record.** `triage_call` (`packages/db/src/schema/oncall/triage-calls.ts`) is "THE after-hours clinical record": nine states, with the open states in `TRIAGE_CALL_OPEN_STATES` (`packages/types/src/oncall.ts`). The transition table is `TRIAGE_CALL_EDGES` in `apps/backend/src/modules/19-oncall/lifecycle/triage-call.ts`. A call has a nullable `patient_id` (a call exists before the caller is identified), a caller kind (`patient`, `caregiver`, `external_clinician`, `portal_session`, `system_inbound`, `bereaved_family`), an inbound channel (`phone`, `sms`, `email`, `portal_message`, `inbound_visit_failure`, `inbound_evv_anomaly`, `inbound_alert_failure`) and a `source` (`manual`, `no_show`, `late_checkout`, `geofence_anomaly`, `m36_delivery_failure`, `paper_origin`).
- **A human opens a call through one route, and the sender of a chat message cannot use it.** `POST /api/v1/triage-calls` (`routes/triage-calls.routes.ts`) needs `triage.open` and the ABAC predicate `oncall_eligible` (an active covering shift, or a supervisor / org-admin / medical-director role, or M17's tonight roster; `abac-predicates.ts`). Its `source` is closed to `manual` (`OpenableSource` in `routes/m19-typebox.ts`) on purpose, so a caller cannot back-date or mislabel a call. The covering shift is resolved server-side, because `on_call_shift_id` is NOT NULL; no covering shift is a 409 that names the gap.
- **Machine-opened calls already exist, and one is M36's.** The D10 consumers (`consumers/inbound-trigger.ts`, `m17-no-show.ts`, `m18-late-checkout.ts`, `m36-delivery-failed.ts`) open a call from an outbox event with `caller_kind = system_inbound`, dedupe on `source_outbox_event_id` (`triage_call_source_dedupe_uq`), coalesce onto an open machine-opened call for the same patient, and, when nothing covers the instant, publish `m19.oncall_shift.coverage_gap` instead of a call. `m36-delivery-failed.ts` opens a patient-less operational call (`source = m36_delivery_failure`, channel `inbound_alert_failure`). That file notes that no producer publishes `m36.notification.delivery_failed` yet, so the consumer is wired but not exercised. This is the precedent for an M36-originated call; there is no precedent for an M36-originated call that carries a patient.
- **M19 SLAs exist and are M19's.** `jobs/triage-sla-policy.ts` and `packages/types/src/oncall.ts`: callback target default 30 minutes (`TRIAGE_CALLBACK_SLA_DEFAULT_MINUTES`, tenant `callback_sla_per_severity`), awaiting-callback grace 60 minutes (tighten-only), per-tier escalation defaults 15 / 30 / 15 / 60 / 15 minutes (`TRIAGE_ESCALATION_SLA_DEFAULT_MINUTES`), with a mandatory escalation capped at the tightest tier default. README decision D6 (`docs/messaging-hub/README.md`; not the decision-sheet D6) (`brief-m19-owner.md`, `brief-clinical-lead-signoff.md`): triage SLAs are untouched and the stricter clock wins where both apply.
- **M19 events M36 could read exist, mostly id-only.** `apps/backend/src/modules/19-oncall/events-by-m19.ts` catalogues `m19.triage_call.opened`, `.disposition_selected`, `.documented`, `.closed`, `.escalation_recorded`, `.callback_sla_breached`, `.escalation_sla_breached` and `m19.oncall_shift.coverage_gap`, all with `declaredConsumers: []` for the triage-call events. `disposition_selected` carries `dispositionData: Type.Unknown()`, so a consumer must not copy it. M36's existing consumer of the SLA and coverage-gap events is `outbox-consumers/oncall-alerts.ts` (alerts only, no thread message).
- **The seam is tier 1 and `rn` only.** The real router (`19-oncall/seams/escalation-router.ts`) answers tier 1 from the covering shift of the patient's provider entity and answers `unavailable` for tier 2 and above. `ESCALATION_DISCIPLINE_BY_ROLE` maps only `rn` to `clinical_rn`; `physician` is `no_route_configured` today, although ADR-0081 lists `physician` as an addressable role. It is `unbound by default` and answers only for organizations in `ESCALATION_ROUTER_ORGS` (`brief-m19-owner.md`).
- **The 4.2 aggregate is per message, per patient, per care-team channel.** `AckRow` / `NewAckRequest` (`apps/backend/src/modules/36-messaging/ack-requests/ack-types.ts`) require `messageId`, `channelId`, `patientId`, one `assignedUserId`, and optionally `addressedRole` (ADR-0081, migration 1129). Acknowledgment is one explicit action by the assigned, still-qualified user; opening or reading never writes (ADR-0069).
- **The escalation walk skips next-tier users who cannot view the channel** (ADR-0070 interim; decision E5). An after-hours on-call who is not on the patient's care team is therefore skipped today.
- **There is no family or caregiver messaging into M36.** Family messaging without an app is WP 6.7, proposed and unbuilt (`10-ui-spec-phases-4-to-6.md` §9). "Family calls" in the 4.13 row can therefore only reach M19 through M19's own intake (phone or portal, M31), not through a chat message.

The failure to design against is hazard H-02 (wrong recipient or nobody), and its twin here: an after-hours item that exists in one module while the other module and the people in it believe it is handled.

## Decision (proposed)

Revised 2026-10-19 by the build team. Everything below is a proposed engineering default, changeable by the product owner; none of it is a decision of a clinical lead, compliance or security.

1. **M19 owns the triage record; M36 only asks, with an id-only event, and never writes M19 tables.** There is no synchronous opener seam and no same-transaction call into M19 (the first draft's `AfterHoursTriageOpener` is dropped). M36 publishes the id-only outbox event `m36.afterhours_triage.requested` inside the urgent send's existing `inTransaction` hook (`urgent/urgent-service.ts`, `inTransaction: async (tx, { messageId })`, around line 216, where `publishUrgentSent` already runs). The send therefore commits or rolls back together with the request event, and M19 is never on the send's critical path. M19's lifecycle, SLAs, shift resolution and escalation rungs are not copied, wrapped or reimplemented in M36.
2. **Two entry paths, kept separate.**
   - **Path A, a staff message after hours (M36 to M19, by event).** A clinician who holds `messaging.oncall.send` addresses the on-call role (ADR-0081). The sender confirms the named person. If it is after hours (Q6 default below) the send additionally publishes `m36.afterhours_triage.requested` for that message. The message and its ack request are exactly the ADR-0081 urgent send; the call is the clinical record, the request is the accountability record.
   - **Path B, a call that arrives in M19's own intake (M19 to M36).** Opened by M19's existing means. M36 surfaces nothing for it in v1 (Q5). A family caller never addresses a role through M36.
3. **Who is addressed, and when the ladder applies.** Path A addresses the person the sender confirmed from the tier-1 resolution (ADR-0081 decisions 3 and 4) through the same guarded router (`guardEscalationRouter`). Role map: `rn` only until M19 maps another role. The fallback ladder (decision 9) applies to **the after-hours triage send only**, not to the ordinary role send. The distinct statuses are: `coverage_gap` (a real router answer that nobody covers), `roster_not_connected` / `router_not_bound` (the deny-stub or an organization off the allowlist; this is a configuration state, **not a coverage gap, and it does NOT trigger the ladder**; the sender is refused with the ADR-0081 telephone-procedure wording), and **`lookup_failed`** (the router or its store failed). A router exception is a 5xx under ADR-0081 (router exceptions propagate); `lookup_failed` is a distinct status shown to the sender and in the audit, **never folded into `coverage_gap`** and never turned into a person. **A fallback-role send opens no triage call** (step 2 of the ladder sends the ordinary urgent message to the confirmed holder of the named role; M19 is not asked).
4. **How the call is opened (M19 side, needs the M19 owner).** A new D10 consumer in M19, `apps/backend/src/modules/19-oncall/consumers/` (working name `m36-afterhours-triage-requested.ts`), modelled on `m36-delivery-failed.ts`: it reads the envelope's organization (never the payload), asserts the patient belongs to it, resolves the covering shift itself, opens a machine-opened call (`caller_kind = system_inbound`, source and channel per Q3), and dedupes on the outbox event id through `triage_call_source_dedupe_uq` (`source_outbox_event_id`), so a redelivery never opens a second call. It applies the existing patient-grain coalesce (a second item for a patient with an open machine-opened call joins it). Nothing covering the instant publishes the existing `m19.oncall_shift.coverage_gap` (already alerted to `org_admin` and `clinical_supervisor` by `oncall-alerts.ts`). M19 then replies with the id-only event `m19.afterhours_triage.outcome` (frozen contract below), always, including when no call was opened, so M36 never waits on silence.
5. **Surfacing the result in the care-team thread.** M36 consumes `m19.afterhours_triage.outcome` (declared consumer m36), finds its link row by `source_outbox_event_id`, records the outcome on the row, and posts at most one fixed-text system line per stage from a closed map: opened, coalesced, no coverage; later stages (disposition, closed) only if Path-A-linked calls are also subscribed from the existing `m19.triage_call.*` events by `triage_call_id`. It never copies `chief_complaint_text`, caller quote, transcript, answers, `dispositionData`, caller phone or protocol content. The line names the call by reference id only. **Q8 default: any disposition wording is withheld in restricted (Part 2) channels until compliance answers**; the thread never becomes the triage record.
6. **Acknowledgment stays the 4.2 aggregate, and the two clocks never merge.** Unchanged from the first draft: acknowledging the request is the only way it leaves `waiting`; opening, working or closing the call does not acknowledge it, and acknowledging does not advance the call. The stricter clock wins and neither is edited (README decision D6). M36's timers and walk (ADR-0070) are unchanged, including the interim skip of a next-tier user who cannot view the channel; the audited thread read of [ADR-0085](ADR-0085-messaging-audited-thread-read-for-escalated-assignee.md) (proposed) is the only way an escalated off-team assignee would see that one thread.
7. **Linking the two records (Q7 default: a table, not a column).** New M36 table `message_afterhours_triage_link`, org-scoped per [ADR-0010](ADR-0010-tenancy-rls-invariants.md) (three-column rule, FORCEd RLS on `organization_id`), one row per after-hours send, written in the same transaction as the message and request: `message_id` UNIQUE (a retry converges), `request_id`, `source_outbox_event_id`, `triage_call_id` NULL until M19 replies, `outcome` NULL until M19 replies. A column on the ack request was rejected: that table's update guard fixes identity and class, and a late reply would have to reopen it. The row is the correlation key for decision 5. **Build constraint:** `HintOutboxPort.publish` (`message-hint-events.ts`) and `OutboxPublishPort.publish` (`bootstrap/outbox-persister.ts`) return `Promise<void>` today, so M36 cannot yet read the id of the event it just published; slice S1 must let the publisher return (or accept) the event id inside the same transaction. This was read, not proven; see Unverified in the delivery report.
8. **Flag, permission, fail-closed.** New tenant flag `messaging.afterhours_triage` (absent = off). It also requires `messaging.role_send`, `messaging.ack_requests`, `messaging.urgent_clinical` and `messaging.sbar`, checked in the service before any router call or publish, so off is the uniform 404. No new permission for Path A (it reuses `messaging.oncall.send`). Flag off: no event is published, no link row written, no thread line posted; M19 behaves exactly as today. If M19 never replies, the request stays `waiting` with its own clock and the link row stays `outcome = NULL`, shown to the sender as "triage call not confirmed" (a visible state, never a silent drop and never a guessed recipient).
9. **Fallback ladder (E4, decided 2026-10-19 in principle; the rules below are proposed build defaults; not built; flag-off).** When the confirmed on-call step cannot proceed, the sender sees an explicit ordered ladder and confirms **each named step**; no step is taken silently and the server never picks a recipient. Step 1: the confirmed on-call (decision 3). Step 2: a role configured per tenant by the tenant admin, in a new M19 column `afterhours_fallback_role` on `tenant_oncall_policy` (nullable, closed to the role list; NULL = step off, fail-closed, shown as unavailable); it sends the ordinary urgent message to the confirmed holder of that role and opens no triage call. Step 3: the per-tenant emergency number, **`tenant_oncall_policy.emergency_contact_e164`, which is BUILT**: migration `0107_oncall_m19_enums_tables.sql` (column at its definition), `packages/db/src/schema/oncall/tenant-oncall-policy.ts` (`emergencyContactE164`), editable through the policy PATCH (`PATCH /api/v1/oncall/policy`, step-up, `routes/shifts.routes.ts`, body field `emergencyContactE164` in `routes/m19-typebox.ts`, written in `routes/write-service-drizzle.ts`), and read for the degraded mode typed in `alerts.ts` (`OncallDegradedAlertFallback`). It is **per (organization, provider entity)** (`tenant_oncall_policy_pe_uq`), and NULL means the step is off. **No migration is needed for step 3.** **Step 3 sends nothing**: the app shows the number to the sender, who dials it; the sender's confirmation that they did so ("I called this number") is an audited event, with no free text. Every step shown, confirmed, refused or unavailable is audited against the patient. The new column for step 2 is the only schema change the ladder needs. This amends the ADR-0081 default for this one purpose; see ADR-0081 decision 3. Clinical-lead concurrence is not recorded.
10. **Audited, time-limited thread read for an escalated assignee (E5).** Specified separately in [ADR-0085](ADR-0085-messaging-audited-thread-read-for-escalated-assignee.md) (proposed), which also amends ADR-0078 decision 7 and ADR-0070 limit (i). Not built; compliance and security concurrence are not recorded; until then the interim skip is the only behaviour.

## Implementation notes (S3 to S5; what was built and where it differs from the text above)

- **Who asks.** While the flag is on, EVERY role send also asks for a triage call: the resolution says so before the sender confirms (`afterhours_triage: true`, a text-and-icon line in the confirm step), the send then publishes the request. M19 decides after-hours-ness (Q6) and answers `no_coverage` when nothing covers; there is no per-send opt-out (an unmade product decision: add one if clinicians should be able to message the on-call RN without opening a call).
- **One publisher, one transaction.** `UrgentSend.requestTriageCall` is set only by the role send. The urgent service's hook creates the response request (`createForUrgentSend` now returns `{ requestId }`), then `afterHoursTriage.requestInTx` publishes `m36.afterhours_triage.requested` with a caller-chosen event id (`OutboxPublishInput.eventId`, S1) and inserts the link row carrying that SAME id. A failing link insert rolls message, request and event back. A replay runs no hook, so it writes nothing more.
- **Statuses.** `coverage_gap` and `roster_not_connected` keep the ADR-0081 422 with the telephone wording and send nothing (no ladder; L2 is not built). With the flag on, a router or store failure is the distinct resolution status `lookup_failed` and a 503 `ROLE_SEND_LOOKUP_FAILED` at send time; with the flag off it stays an unmapped 5xx exactly as ADR-0081 says. An unreadable flag is a 5xx, never read as off.
- **Outcome consumer** (`outbox-consumers/afterhours-outcome.ts`, consumer `m36.afterhours-outcome`, bound in `apps/backend/src/index.ts` beside the promotion consumers): records the outcome from the ENVELOPE organization, regardless of the flag (an answer M19 already committed is kept). The same outcome again converges. A malformed payload, an unknown source event id or a DIFFERENT second outcome throws, so the outbox retries and then dead-letters it. The deferral entry for `m19.afterhours_triage.outcome` in `scripts/outbox-deferred-consumers.ts` was deleted.
- **Thread line (deviation, deliberate).** The line is not a stored system message: it is DERIVED at read time from the link row through the closed map `AFTERHOURS_TRIAGE_LINE_TEXT` (`packages/types`), served by `GET /api/v1/messaging/channels/:id/afterhours-triage?message_ids=` (messaging.read + channel visibility; flag off is the uniform 404). "At most one line per stage" holds because the link records the outcome once; there is no free text to scrub and no second record in the chat. In a restricted (Part 2: the patient carries the `sud` privacy flag) channel the outcome wording is withheld (`withheld`); a restriction lookup that fails is read as restricted. An unanswered request reads "Triage call not confirmed". The later stages (disposition, closed) are NOT shown: no `m19.triage_call.*` subscription was added (Q5, Q8).
- **Web.** The card line is mounted through the acknowledgment-request provider (`afterhours-triage-line.tsx`); it asks only for role-addressed requests and shows nothing when the flag is off or the read fails. The wording is PROVISIONAL (clinical-lead sign-off pending).
- **Live suite.** `apps/backend/__tests__/m36-afterhours-send.live.test.ts` (`test:m36:afterhours-send:live`, CI row added) over the real catalog-validating publisher and a fake bound router: it proves the atomic commit, the link id equal to the committed outbox row id, replay, rollback, the statuses, the consumer and the line. It does NOT run M19's real consumer end to end (that is S6).
- **Restricted (Part 2) channels, review fix (OPEN COMPLIANCE QUESTION).** (1) The thread-line read withholds EVERY link row of a restricted channel, an unanswered request included (it used to read "not confirmed"), and the restriction is read in the same step as the rows, so a member cannot learn from the wording that a triage request exists; a lookup that cannot say is read as restricted. The presence of a withheld line still shows that a row exists: whether even that is acceptable is for compliance. Soft-deleted messages carry no line. (2) The urgent send does not emit `m36.afterhours_triage.requested` (which names the patient) for a restricted channel or when the restriction lookup throws: the message and its response request still commit, no event and no link are written, and the sender gets the plain send result (the same silent skip as the flag being off; no refusal, no extra message). Whether a Part 2 patient's id may ever travel in that event to M19, and whether the sender must be told no call was requested, are OPEN compliance and product questions; this default fails closed until they are answered.

## What changes in M19 (needs the named owner, E1)

Proposed; (a) and (b) are BUILT as slice S2 (unwired, see Status), (c) and (d) are not: (a) the D10 consumer of `m36.afterhours_triage.requested` and the publication of `m19.afterhours_triage.outcome` (catalogue entries in `events-by-m19.ts`, `declaredConsumers: ["m36"]`); (b) the `triage_call_source` and channel values (Q3), an enum and CHECK change in M19's schema; (c) slice L1, the nullable `afterhours_fallback_role` column and its policy PATCH field; (d) if Path B correlation is ever wanted, declare M36 a consumer of the triage-call events (`declaredConsumers` is empty today). **Unchanged:** the nine-state machine and `TRIAGE_CALL_EDGES`, `triage.open` and `oncall_eligible` on the human route, the closed `OpenableSource`, the SLA crons, their defaults and tighten-only rules, the mandatory-escalation ceiling, the D10 dedupe and coalesce, the coverage-gap watchdog. Triage SLAs are untouched; where an M36 deadline and an M19 SLA both apply the stricter wins ([`brief-m19-owner.md`](../messaging-hub/brief-m19-owner.md), README decision D6).

## PHI, tenancy and RLS

- Tenant comes from the request context (Path A) or the outbox envelope (both consumers), never from a payload or body field. The M19 consumer asserts that the patient belongs to the envelope's organization; a cross-org or unknown patient is a visible `no_coverage`-class refusal path, not a guess.
- The link table is tenant-scoped under the three-column rule with FORCEd RLS ([ADR-0010](ADR-0010-tenancy-rls-invariants.md)); `arch:tenancy`, `arch:table-ownership` and the manifest `.ts` and `.cjs` mirror apply.
- Ids, enums and instants only cross the events; message text, SBAR and clinical free text never reach M19, and nothing M19 denylists comes back. Logs carry the organization and a status word, never a patient, name, text or recipient.
- Out-of-band notices keep the ADR-0073 / WP 4.7 rule: no patient identifiers in a push, SMS or email.
- Live suites prove RLS as the real `wardenx_app` NOBYPASSRLS role on a private database (repo rule) and use the real catalog-validating publisher, not `NOOP_OUTBOX`.

## Hazards (listed here; not added to the register in `docs/messaging-hub/05-clinical-workflows-and-safety.md`)

The register's H-01 to H-10 are fixed by that document; this ADR does not extend it. Proposed new hazards for the clinical lead to accept into the register, or reject:

- **A. Two records, one believed handled.** The call is worked in M19 while the request stays `waiting` (or the reverse). Mitigation: decision 6 keeps both clocks live and the unacknowledged queue (WP 4.9) still shows the request.
- **B. Off-team on-call skipped (E5).** The after-hours on-call is usually not on the care team, so the walk skips them. Mitigation: the interim skip stays the default; the on-call works the call in M19; the audited thread read is specified in ADR-0085 (proposed, unbuilt, no recorded concurrence), so the request ack for an off-team user stays blocked until it is built.
- **C. Wrong "after hours".** Mitigation: the sender sees before sending whether a triage call will be requested; the determination is M19 shift truth (Q6), not an M36 clock.
- **D. Disposition shown in a thread to people who should not see it.** Mitigation: fixed wording from the enum only, withheld in restricted channels (Q8 default).
- **E. Duplicate calls on retry or redelivery (H-09 family).** Mitigation: `message_id` UNIQUE on the link, the event-id dedupe `triage_call_source_dedupe_uq`, and M19's patient-grain coalesce.
- **F. Messaging as the emergency path (H-03).** The permanent "not the emergency path" notice stays on the composer.
- **G. A lost reply.** M19 never answers, so the sender believes a call exists. Mitigation: decision 8 (the link stays `outcome = NULL` and is shown as "triage call not confirmed"); the request and its clock are unaffected.

## Alternatives rejected

- **A synchronous opener seam called inside the send transaction (the first draft).** Couples the send's commit to a cross-module call, needs a deny-stub and guard in `packages/types`, and a slow or failing M19 would block or fail a clinical send. The event and reply pattern is the one `m36-delivery-failed.ts` already uses.
- **M36 writes `triage_call` rows directly.** Violates M19 ownership and ADR-0011 D1.
- **Let the sender call `POST /api/v1/triage-calls`.** Gated on `oncall_eligible`, which a chat sender is not; widening it changes who can open a clinical record.
- **Merge the ack deadline and the triage SLA into one clock.** Contradicts README decision D6.
- **Automatic or silent fallback to tier 2, a manager, the care-team primary or a telephone number.** A widened page the sender does not see and confirm is a silent change of who is paged (H-02). An explicit, sender-confirmed ladder is allowed (decision 9); a silent one stays rejected.
- **Triggering the ladder on `roster_not_connected` / `router_not_bound`.** That state means the tenant has no router, not that a shift is uncovered; laddering there would route every send to fallbacks for a tenant that never configured on-call.
- **Folding `lookup_failed` into `coverage_gap`.** Hides a failing lookup as a real gap and contradicts ADR-0081 (router exceptions are 5xx).
- **A link column on the ack request.** See decision 7.
- **Close the triage call when the request is acknowledged, or the reverse.** Acknowledging a message is not a clinical disposition.
- **Copy the call's content or disposition detail into the thread.** Makes chat a second, unscrubbed clinical record.
- **Family messaging through M36 now.** WP 6.7 is unbuilt; it needs its own ADR and a privacy review.

- **S3, M36 send path.** The flagged after-hours extension of the role send: publish the request event in the `inTransaction` hook, insert the link row, the `lookup_failed` / `roster_not_connected` / `coverage_gap` statuses, the outcome consumer that fills the link. **Edits `urgent/urgent-service.ts` and `oncall-directory/role-send-service.ts`.** Depends on S1 (and S2 for the live acceptance).
- **S4, thread lines.** Fixed-text lines from a closed map, restricted-channel withholding (Q8), redelivery converges, malformed event to DLQ; live suite that the line lands and carries no content. Depends on S3.

## Consequences (if accepted)

- Two catalogued events, one M19 consumer, one M36 consumer, one link table, one flag; M36 migrations for the link table (and G1); M19 migrations for the source enum (Q3) and the fallback column (L1). No change to the ack aggregate's state machine or to M19's triage lifecycle and SLAs.
- Until a router is bound for a tenant, the feature answers "roster not connected" and refuses, which is the honest behaviour.

## Known ceilings

- Tier 1 and `rn` only, as the router stands; the sender confirms one person. The E4 ladder and the E5 thread read are not built.
- The check-then-send window of ADR-0081 decision 4 applies, plus one more: the call is opened asynchronously after the send commits, so the shift could change in between; the call's `on_call_shift_id` is M19's resolution at consume time, not the sender's.
- The reply is asynchronous: the sender sees "triage call not confirmed" until M19 answers.
- The thread line is informational and is only as complete as the events M19 publishes; `dispositionData` is deliberately not read.
- Live acceptance needs the real router bound for a test organization (`ESCALATION_ROUTER_ORGS`).

## Still blocked or open after S3 to S5 (not decided here)

- A named, permanent M19 owner and a bound router (E1); until then every send answers `roster_not_connected` and refuses. S6 (live acceptance across a shift boundary and DST through the real router) is blocked on it.
- L1 (`afterhours_fallback_role`), L2 and L3 (the ladder): L1 is an M19 column that needs the owner; L2 edits the same two files as S3 and depends on L1; clinical-lead concurrence on E4 is not recorded.
- G2 and G3 (grant wiring, thread read): E5 compliance and security concurrence and the E5 design questions are open ([ADR-0085](ADR-0085-messaging-audited-thread-read-for-escalated-assignee.md)). Until then an off-team on-call user is still skipped by the escalation walk, so their request cannot be acknowledged.
- Product questions this build did not answer: whether a role send may opt out of the triage request; whether later call stages (disposition, closed) appear in the thread; the final wording of the lines and the indicator.
