# ADR-0097 — Messaging acknowledge-required broadcast: an announcement that needs an explicit, per-person, idempotent acknowledgment, with a who-has-not-acknowledged list scoped to the frozen audience

- **Status:** **Proposed** (2026-10-23). Backend, migration 1200 and the screen are built flag-off (see Implementation status). The audience rule, who may oversee, the late-joiner policy and the absence of reminders are the build team's defaults; compliance and clinical-operations concurrence is not recorded.
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Phase:** Messaging Hub Phase 6, WP 6.8 (extends TC-08 / WP 2.9 announcements).
- **Related:** [ADR-0056](ADR-0056-clinical-acknowledgment-vs-read-receipts.md) (a read receipt is never an acknowledgment), [ADR-0069](ADR-0069-messaging-acknowledgment-request-aggregate.md) (the WP 4.2 aggregate, considered and not reused: D1), [ADR-0073](ADR-0073-messaging-notification-policy.md) (lock-screen rules a later reminder must obey: D9), [ADR-0078](ADR-0078-messaging-supervisor-reassign.md), [ADR-0092](ADR-0092-messaging-response-analytics.md) (no per-person league tables: D8), [ADR-0010](ADR-0010-tenancy-rls-invariants.md), `docs/messaging-hub/04-phases-4-to-6.md` (WP 6.8), `10-ui-spec-phases-4-to-6.md` (Broadcast).

## Context

WP 6.8: "Announcement (policy change, weather closure) that requires acknowledgment, with a who-has-not-read list for supervisors; extends TC-08. Guardrail: supervisor sees every non-reader; ack is an explicit action, never implied by open." Today an announcement (org-wide channel, org_admin-only send, `messaging.announce`) records a `read` receipt when a person opens it (WP 2.9) and the sender can list readers and unread. That answers "was it displayed", which is exactly what ADR-0056 says a receipt must not be taken to mean. A closure notice or a policy change needs a stronger, deliberate statement, and the people responsible need the complete list of who has not made it.

## Decision (proposed)

1. **D1: a separate lightweight per-recipient row, not the WP 4.2 aggregate.** `message_acknowledgment_request` (ADR-0069) is one message, one *assigned* clinician, a patient, an ack class, a deadline, an escalation chain, reassignment, delivery-failure handling and supervisor queues. A broadcast is one message, N recipients, no patient, no assignee choice, no deadline and (in this slice) no escalation. Forcing N requests through the aggregate would create N urgent-queue entries, N escalation timers and N outbox event streams for a notice that is not clinically urgent, and it would flood the supervisor's unacknowledged-urgent queue (WP 4.9) with weather notices, burying the urgent clinical requests that queue exists for. Two small tables instead (migration 1200): `message_ack_broadcast` (one row per announcement that requires acknowledgment) and `message_ack_broadcast_recipient` (the frozen audience, `acknowledged_at`). What is reused is the *doctrine* of ADR-0056/0069: the acknowledgment is an explicit act by the person, stamped by the database clock, never inferred.
2. **D2: who can require acknowledgment.** Only the **author** of a live message in the announcement channel (and the author must hold `messaging.announce`, which is who can post there). Marking is a separate explicit action after the announcement exists (`POST /announcements/:messageId/ack-broadcast`), idempotent per announcement. Everything else (unknown, someone else's, deleted, not an announcement) is one 404. The migration guard repeats the author and channel-kind check, so a forged insert is refused by the database.
3. **D3: the audience is frozen at publish: every active member of the organization except the author.** "Active" is membership `active` and user `active` at the instant of marking, snapshotted into recipient rows by the same transaction. A tenant above `ACK_BROADCAST_MAX_AUDIENCE = 5000` active members is refused with a typed 422 rather than given a partial audience. The audience is org-wide because the announcement channel is org-wide (it has no member rows); narrowing by role or location needs a source of truth for team membership that M36 does not own (the same reason ADR-0092 left team breakdowns out) and is deferred.
4. **D4: later joiners are not asked, but are not hidden.** Someone who joins after publish is not in the audience (so a closure notice is not demanded of a new hire a month later) and has no row, hence the uniform 404 if they try to acknowledge. The supervisor view carries `outsideAudience`, the count of currently active staff not in the audience, and the screen says so in words. Adding them (an explicit "ask the people who joined since") is a later action; it was not built because it needs a decision on whether a policy notice should follow a joiner automatically.
5. **D5: who sees the who-has-not-acknowledged list: the broadcast's CREATOR only.** The route permission is `messaging.announce`, and on top of it the detail and list reads are scoped to broadcasts the caller created (`message_ack_broadcast.created_by = caller`); anyone else, including another `messaging.announce` holder, gets the same uniform 404 as an unknown id, and `GET /ack-broadcasts` lists only the caller's own broadcasts. Parity with the WP 2.9 receipts list was considered and rejected: that list (`/announcements/:messageId/receipts`) is org-wide for every `messaging.announce` holder with no documented reason for it, and this slice names individual staff who have *not* acknowledged, which is a more sensitive fact than an unread count; copying an org-wide reach would let any announcer inspect a colleague's chase. Consequence: nobody above the author can see a broadcast's non-acknowledgers yet (no org_admin or compliance override), so a manager cannot take over when the author leaves; that is open item 1. The list is the frozen audience of *that* broadcast only, carries display names, state and instants, never message text, and a soft-deleted announcement's broadcast is hidden from it. A "supervisor" below org_admin is not given anything: there is no reporting-line source of truth in M36 to scope "my people". Every read is accounted through the read audit keyed on the message.
6. **D6: acknowledgment is explicit, per person, idempotent and on the database clock.** `POST /announcements/:messageId/ack` has no body and no user field: the actor is the server-side request context. The recipient row's only possible change is `acknowledged_at` NULL to the database `now()`, by that person (guard trigger `message_ack_broadcast_recipient_guard`; the app role has `UPDATE (acknowledged_at)` only and no DELETE). A repeat returns 200 with the original instant and `already: true`; an acknowledgment is never withdrawn. Opening an announcement writes a `message_receipt` and nothing else: the two facts are stored apart and a live test asserts opening leaves `acknowledged_at` NULL. The author cannot acknowledge (not in the audience) and nobody can acknowledge for another person (no field, and the guard checks `app.user_id`). Acknowledging does not require having opened first (a person may have read it elsewhere); the screen puts the open link beside the button and says opening is not acknowledging.
7. **D7: every non-acknowledger is listed; offboarded recipients are listed apart.** The detail list is paged by exact offset (default 100, max 200) with counts over the whole audience, so nothing is silently truncated (the older receipts list capped at 500 per list and said so; this one does not). Per recipient the state is derived live: `acknowledged`; else `ineligible` (membership or user no longer active: offboarded, suspended); else `opened`; else `not_opened`. `pending` = opened + not opened, the people to chase. An ineligible person is never counted among the pending (nobody is chased who cannot act) and never dropped (the count and the filter show them), so "audience = acknowledged + pending + ineligible" always holds. A person who acknowledged and then left stays `acknowledged`, because that happened.
8. **D8: purpose limit.** The per-person view exists to chase an organisational notice, not to rank staff. It is limited to that one broadcast, is read-audited, and there is no cross-broadcast per-person aggregate, no export and no analytics feed (ADR-0092's rule against league tables applies; any such view needs the same HR/compliance review).
9. **D9: no outbound dispatch in this slice.** No push, SMS, email or in-app reminder is sent when acknowledgment is required or as time passes. The existing notification policy (ADR-0073) is built around per-recipient routing for clinical urgency and quiet hours; sending N reminders for a non-clinical notice through it was not reused because the policy's recipients, urgency classes and delivery-failure escalation are defined for acknowledgment *requests* (WP 4.2), not for a fan-out announcement, and bolting on a second path would bypass its quiet-hours and lock-screen guarantees. Therefore reminders are **not built**. When they are: text must be fixed and generic ("You have an announcement to acknowledge"), never the announcement text, a name or a patient (ADR-0073 lock-screen rules), routed through the policy, and rate-limited per recipient. The announcement itself still reaches people through the channel as today.
10. **D10: rate limit and step-up.** All five routes carry a per-route budget (20/min/user, 200/min/org, fail-open: an availability control, not the privacy one). Step-up is **not** required: acknowledging is a low-risk self act, and marking is no more sensitive than posting the announcement (which already needs `messaging.announce`); the data the list reveals (names and a yes/no for a non-clinical notice) is not worth a second factor on every refresh. Revisit if a tenant uses the list for compliance attestation (open item 4).
11. **D11: flag.** Per-tenant flag `messaging.ack_broadcast` through the existing `feature_flag` mechanism (absent = off), read on every call (a kill switch lands at once) and checked after the permission and rate limit but **before the request is parsed**: off is a uniform 404, identical for a malformed id and a malformed query, so a bad request cannot tell a flag-off tenant from a flag-on one. Existing rows survive the flag going off (nothing is deleted) and become reachable again when it returns. No new permission.
12. **D12: audit.** Both tables carry the 1107 audit trigger. Rows hold ids and instants; they contain no name, text or patient, so the audit trail cannot leak the announcement. Creating a broadcast inserts one recipient row per audience member in one transaction, so it writes up to `ACK_BROADCAST_MAX_AUDIENCE` (5000) audit rows in that transaction. That is expected and accepted: the audit trigger stays per row (batching it would weaken the per-row trail), the cap bounds the cost, and creation is a rare, author-initiated act under the route budget (D10).

## Hazards

- **A compliance reader treats "acknowledged" as "understood".** It means the person pressed the button. The words on the screen say so; it is not training attestation.
- **Audience drift.** The frozen audience is a snapshot; D4 says who it misses. A person who is added to the org and is active at marking but whose membership was wrongly `pending` is silently outside until the late-joiner action exists.
- **Marking after people have already read.** The audience is the active staff at marking time, not at send time; readers before marking still need to acknowledge (they are `opened`).
- **Immutable by design.** A broadcast cannot be withdrawn or edited in this slice (no UPDATE grant); a mistaken broadcast stays. The announcement's own delete (WP 2.9) leaves the broadcast referencing a deleted message; the author guard is evaluated only at insert.
- **Screen identifies announcements by sent time.** The text is encrypted at rest and this slice adds no decrypting reader to the broadcast list, so a supervisor sees "Announcement of 23 Oct, 10:00" with an open link to the channel, not a title.

Built on the WP 6.8 branch: `packages/types/src/messaging-ack-broadcast.ts`, migration `1200_m36_ack_broadcast.sql`, Drizzle schema `message-ack-broadcast.ts`, manifest entries (`.ts` and `.cjs`), `apps/backend/src/modules/36-messaging/ack-broadcast/ack-broadcast-service.ts`, routes `routes/ack-broadcast-routes.ts` and TypeBox schemas, route budget `ACK_BROADCAST_ROUTE_RATE_LIMIT`, boot wiring (flag via the platform reader), regenerated OpenAPI and typed client, web screen `/messaging/broadcasts` (`ack-broadcast.tsx`, hooks, wording, CSS, DOM tests with axe), a "Require acknowledgment" action in the announcement receipts dialog, live suite `test:m36:ack-broadcast:live` (CI row "M36 ack broadcast") as the real `chartwarden_app` role, a migration-pin test and unit tests.

## Not done / open

1. **Supervisor oversight.** Today only the broadcast's creator sees its non-acknowledgers (D5). Letting an org_admin or compliance role see any broadcast (for coverage when the author leaves), or a unit supervisor see the non-acknowledgers of *their own* people, needs a deliberate permission and a reporting-line or team source of truth.
2. **Reminders and any outbound notification** (D9), including the lock-screen-safe wording and the choice of policy class.
3. **Late-joiner action, role/location-narrowed audiences, a deadline or due date, withdraw/close, an announcement title or text excerpt in the list.**
4. Whether tenants will use this as a compliance attestation (then a step-up, a retention class for the rows and an export need their own decision), and the HR/compliance review of D8.
5. Clinical-operations and compliance concurrence on D3 to D5; switching the flag on for any tenant.
