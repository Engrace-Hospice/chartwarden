# ADR-0099 — Messaging family/caregiver messaging without an app: a consented, bearer-link contact principal that writes inbound-only into a dedicated family inbox, never into staff channels

- **Status:** **Proposed** (2026-10-23), **build in progress behind the tenant flag `messaging.family_messaging` (absent = off) and fail-closed without a validated `TRUSTED_PROXY_CIDRS` and a code pepper** (slices S0 to S5 on `feat/m36-wp67-family-messaging`; see Implementation status). The privacy officer, counsel (BAA, consumer-messaging law) and clinical-lead decisions below are NOT recorded. Every "D" is the build team's default unless marked "DECISION NEEDED"; each such value is implemented as a named constant or tenant setting and is listed under Open questions. Nothing is sent automatically (D17: S7 is not built). The ADR stays Proposed: the gate of WP 6.7 ("privacy officer + new ADR before build") is a human sign-off this build did not obtain, so the feature must not be switched on for a tenant until it is recorded.
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Phase:** Messaging Hub Phase 6, WP 6.7 ("Family/caregiver messaging, no app").
- **Related:** [ADR-0096](ADR-0096-messaging-external-partner-share.md) (the bearer principal, link plus out-of-band code, uniform 404, no-cache revocation reused here), [ADR-0073](ADR-0073-messaging-notification-policy.md) and [ADR-0074](ADR-0074-messaging-lock-screen-privacy.md) (platform floor, no preview in push), [ADR-0086](ADR-0086-messaging-retention-and-legal-hold.md) (retention, legal hold), [ADR-0093](ADR-0093-messaging-multilingual-ui-and-templates.md) (language), [ADR-0094](ADR-0094-messaging-voice-notes-and-dictation.md) and [ADR-0095](ADR-0095-messaging-ai-summaries-and-drafting.md) (style, BAA posture), [ADR-0010](ADR-0010-tenancy-rls-invariants.md), [ADR-0055](ADR-0055-message-revisions-and-redaction.md), `docs/messaging-hub/04-phases-4-to-6.md` row 6.7, `docs/messaging-hub/05-clinical-workflows-and-safety.md`, `docs/messaging-hub/11-decision-sheet.md` (section F), M36 spec (non-goals and channel table), M31 spec (portal account model), M5 spec (`caregiver`, `patient_caregiver`), M16 (bereavement), M39 (consent).

## Context

WP 6.7: "One-time link + code, no PHI in SMS/email, routed to the care team; bereavement channel kept after death." Gate: "New outbound surface: privacy officer + new ADR before build."

Facts read from the repo:

- **A portal path already exists and is the default for families.** M31 owns the patient/family/caregiver/bereaved-family portal (`apps/portal`): a `portal_account` ties an M2 `user` to a patient through a consent-gated `portal_access_grant`; sessions are the M2 `portal` class (30-minute idle, 8-hour hard expiry, TOTP step-up); onboarding is a magic link plus password plus 2FA. Messaging from the portal is one of M31's four write paths and lands as `channel_member.role = 'portal_caregiver'` in the patient's `care_team` channel (M36 spec channel table: "portal caregivers added via M39 consent"). `apps/portal` today holds the sign-in, shell, patient context and screens; this ADR did not verify how much of the M36 portal-messaging path is built, and does not change it.
- **WP 6.7 is for the person who will not make an account**: an adult child on a phone, a neighbour, a spouse who only texts. They have no `user` row and, as in ADR-0096, must never get one as a side effect (a user row carries roles, a default organisation and the whole `messaging.*` surface).
- **The data model for who they are already exists in M5**: `caregiver` and `patient_caregiver` (`relationship`, `is_primary`, `is_legal_representative`, `lr_authority` of `dpoa_healthcare|guardian|next_of_kin|none`, `lr_proof_doc_ref`, `portal_invitation_consented`, `involvement_from/to`). M39 owns consent, with kinds including `family_caregiver_access`, `dpoa_healthcare` and `personal_representative_authority_post_death`. M16 owns the bereavement plan; on death M5 flips `patient.status` to `deceased` and bereavement access follows M16.
- **M36's spec treats portal caregivers and bereavement family as in-scope participants** ("internal: staff + portal caregivers + bereavement family") but excludes bulk messaging to external recipients. This ADR stays inside that line: one-to-one, reply-driven, never a broadcast.
- **No PHI out of band is a standing rule** (ADR-0073/0074 floor, ADR-0096 D9). M36's spec names Twilio SMS and Postmark email dispatchers; this ADR did not verify what is wired or whether a BAA exists (ADR-0073 and the decision sheet row A2 say no sending service exists in any environment).
- **The partner-share machinery (ADR-0096) is the nearest precedent and is not generic**: its tables, session class and routes are partner-specific, and it is built behind `messaging.external_partners`. This ADR reuses the *pattern* and, where sensible, shared primitives (token hashing, staged session, uniform 404, trusted-proxy gate), not the partner tables.

The hazard is the inverse of ADR-0096: a partner reads staff content; here a lay person writes into a clinical record, can be impersonated, can be a different person than the one consented, and may be grieving. The default is therefore the smallest surface that serves the need: a verified person sends text to a dedicated, staffed inbox, and staff answer through an approved path.

## Decision (proposed)

1. **D1: a new principal, the family contact, that is neither a `user` nor a `portal_account`.** A *family contact access principal* is a bearer-token session bound to exactly one `patient_caregiver` row (so one person for one patient), minted through the staged flow in D3. It carries no roles and no permissions; the only thing it can do is the four routes in D4. It is deliberately **not** an M31 portal session: the portal's contract is "account with password and 2FA", and bending it into "a link that sometimes works" would weaken every portal guarantee. **Relationship to the portal (DECISION NEEDED, privacy officer + product owner):** the build default is that the portal stays the preferred path and the link path is the fallback. The staff screen offers "invite to the portal" first and "send a one-time message link" second; a contact who later creates a portal account has the link principal revoked and the conversation continues in the care-team channel under M31 (D5 migration of history is NOT done: the family inbox stays as it was).
2. **D2: who may be a contact: only a person the patient (or their personal representative) has consented to.** Eligibility is checked live, in the same statement as every request: an M5 `patient_caregiver` row that is current (`involvement_to` null or future), and an M39 consent of kind `family_caregiver_access` (or, where `is_legal_representative`, the matching representative consent) that is active and covers messaging. A revoked consent, an ended involvement, or a patient restriction ends access on the next request (D12). **No consent, no link: the staff screen refuses to create the invitation**, with a reason code, not a bypass. `paid_caregiver` and `friend` relationships are eligible only if the consent names them. **HIPAA personal representative (DECISION NEEDED, privacy officer + counsel):** the default reading is that a contact with `is_legal_representative = true` and a valid `lr_proof_doc_ref` is treated as the patient for the scope of the consent, and a contact without it is a person the patient permitted under 45 CFR 164.510(b) (involvement in care), which allows disclosure of relevant information but is not a right of access. The module does not decide this; the consent record does, and counsel states which relationships qualify in each state.
3. **D3: link plus code, staged, modelled on ADR-0096 D5/D13/D14.** Staff create an invitation for one contact. The platform returns a one-time link (256-bit token, `sha256` at rest, single use, atomic redeem) and a code that staff must give the contact through a *different* channel than the link. **The code travels by voice (a call from the care team) by default**; it is never in the same SMS or email as the link. Redeeming the link mints an *enrolment-stage* session reaching only the verify route and the state route; five wrong codes lock the invitation (a CHECK-bounded counter that only a staff reissue resets, with a new link and a new code); on success the session moves to `active` and its bearer is replaced. Session life: 30 idle minutes, 12 hours absolute, as D13 of ADR-0096; a contact whose session ended asks for a new link (D8). The code length is a **DECISION NEEDED (security)**: ADR-0096 uses 8 digits and records the offline brute-force exposure of a leaked hash (its limit l); the default here is 8 digits plus a per-invitation pepper held outside the table, and TOTP is not offered (the point of the feature is no app).
4. **D4: what the principal can do: four routes, all fixed to its own conversation.** (a) *state* (is access live, display name of the agency, language); (b) *verify*; (c) *read own thread*: the messages of its own family inbox, nothing else; (d) *send*: a text message of at most 2,000 characters, no attachments, no links rendered, no mentions, into its own family inbox. The conversation id is read from the invitation row in the same statement as the session, never from the request. No listing of contacts, members, staff or other threads. No read receipts that expose staff presence; a delivery state of "received by the care team" only after a staff member has the message in a worked queue.
5. **D5: inbound-only versus two-way: two-way, but replies are staff-initiated and bounded, and nothing is real time.** The contact sends; the care team replies from the staff inbox as ordinary messages in the family thread (D6). There is no typing indicator, no presence, no push to the contact with content. The contact sees a reply when they open the link and read the thread. **Inbound-only was rejected as the default** (a one-way tip box that never answers fails the stated purpose and is the pattern families abandon) and **fully real-time two-way was rejected** (it implies an availability the care team cannot promise). An auto-reply states the service is not monitored for emergencies (D14).
6. **D6: routing into M36 without exposing staff-only content: a new channel kind, `family`, not the existing `care_team` or `bereavement` channels.** Each (patient, contact) pair gets one conversation of a new kind `family`, created lazily at first send. Staff members who are on the patient's care team (the same `CareTeamProvider` and ABAC predicate the care-team channel uses) see it in a "Family messages" lane of the smart inbox (ADR-0091); the contact sees only this one thread. **Why not write into `care_team`:** that channel carries staff clinical discussion, SBAR, mentions, acknowledgments and promotion requests, and ADR-0096 D1 already refuses to expose it to a non-workforce reader. A contact posting into it would also make every staff message in it a message the contact can read. Staff-only content never reaches the contact because the contact's reader is scoped to `family`-kind conversations only, by construction and by test. **A reply into the family thread is a message authored by a staff user to a family thread; it is not a chart note**, and the composer carries a persistent line stating that the contact can read it. A staff member can *promote* a contact message to the chart (the existing M36 promotion path, unchanged), which is how clinical content from a contact becomes part of the record. **Bereavement:** while the plan is open and the contact is a named bereaved contact, the same contact principal can reach the M16 bereavement counsellor through a `family` conversation; the M36 `bereavement` channel (a staff channel with its own rules) is not exposed. This reading of the spec's "bereavement counselor + named bereaved family member(s) via M39 consent" membership is **a DECISION NEEDED (bereavement coordinator + privacy officer)**: either the contact joins the existing `bereavement` channel as a `portal_caregiver`-style member (the M36 spec reading, but it exposes the whole channel to the contact and so needs the channel's staff content to be separated), or it stays a `family` thread with the counsellor as the staff side (the build default, safer, and the same behaviour to the contact).
7. **D7: no PHI in SMS or email; lock-screen rules.** Any SMS or email to a contact uses one fixed string that takes no argument: for example "You have a new secure message from your care team. Open your link to read it." It contains no patient name, no agency detail that identifies hospice care (the agency name itself can reveal the diagnosis class, so **the default is a neutral sender name chosen by the tenant, DECISION NEEDED privacy officer**), no clinician name, no count, no preview, and never the code or the link in the same message as each other. The link in the *invitation* message is delivered by the care team through the channel the contact has on file and prefers; the code is spoken. The fixed string is registered as one template in the ADR-0093 template store (one per locale, D17), reviewed once, and the sender function accepts no free text. Consistent with ADR-0074 the contact has no push app and no lock-screen preview exists; the SMS above *is* the lock-screen surface, and it is therefore the least informative string in the system.
8. **D8: rate limits and abuse.** Per client address and per invitation, using the ADR-0096 trusted-proxy gate (**fail closed**: the feature is off unless `TRUSTED_PROXY_CIDRS` validates, ADR-0096 D17). Defaults (all build-team defaults, not agreed values): redeem and verify limited per address; code attempts 5 per invitation; send 10 messages per hour and 40 per day per contact; a thread holds at most 200 messages from one contact before staff must reply or reset; an organisation holds at most 25 live invitations per patient care team per day, a creator at most 10. **Spam and harassment:** a staff member can *close* a family thread (the contact's send then answers a neutral "this conversation is closed, please call the agency") and *block* a contact (revokes the invitation and prevents reissue without a second authoriser). A contact who sends more than the per-hour budget gets a 429 with a plain message and the staff lane shows a "high volume" chip. Messages are never auto-deleted for spam; content a staff member judges abusive is retained (D13) and flagged, not purged.
9. **D9: what staff see, and who may invite.** New permission `messaging.family.manage` (create, reissue, revoke, close, block) held by the clinical supervisor, social work and bereavement coordinator roles and the compliance officer, granted by a future migration to those roles only (names to be confirmed against the role catalogue, **DECISION NEEDED clinical lead**); a separate `messaging.family.respond` for staff who may reply, held by the care-team disciplines. Creating or reissuing an invitation requires a fresh step-up and the staff member to be able to view the patient (the plain care-team predicate, no break-glass, as ADR-0096 D14). Staff see, per contact: relationship, consent state, link state (not yet opened, active, locked, expired, revoked), last contact activity, unanswered-message age. The unanswered family message participates in the same queue discipline as other work items but is **not** an acknowledgment request and **never** triggers ADR-0079 escalation or paging by default (a family text is not an emergency path; D14).
10. **D10: consent, Part 2 and sensitive categories.** Sending and reading require D2 live. **42 CFR Part 2:** a patient whose record is Part 2 restricted and not released to this person (by a Part 2-compliant consent) has **no family conversation created at all**; the staff screen shows a reason code, not a hidden thread. A Part 2 restriction added after the fact suspends the contact's read and send immediately (uniform 404, D12) and flags open threads. A message *from* the contact is the contact's own words and is not a Part 2 disclosure by the program, but a *reply* that quotes or confirms restricted information is, so the reply composer runs the existing restricted-patient checks used for care-team replies and refuses to post to a contact the restriction does not cover. The family thread is never a source for AI summaries or drafts (ADR-0095 is scoped to staff-authorised threads) and never for global search by non-participants.
11. **D11: language.** The contact's preferred language (M5 `patient_communication_preference`, falling back to the tenant default) selects the locale of the fixed string, the contact-facing page chrome and the auto-reply (ADR-0093 catalogue, new keys under a `family.*` namespace, reviewed by a qualified translator before any locale is switched on). **Free-text messages are never machine-translated for the contact in this slice** (the ADR-0093 posture on clinical content applies); staff see the contact's language in the thread header and may use the existing interpreter workflow. Right-to-left and plain-reading-level review are part of the translation sign-off.
12. **D12: revocation is instant because nothing is cached; uniform refusals.** Every request resolves token, invitation, eligibility (D2) and thread in one SQL statement on the database clock: `revoked_at IS NULL AND expires_at > now() AND consent active AND involvement current AND patient not Part 2-blocked`. No grant cache, no JWT. Revoke needs only the manage permission and a step-up and never depends on the flag. Unknown, expired, revoked, locked, wrong stage, flag off, wrong tenant and bad token all answer the same neutral 404 (ADR-0096 D12); the page shows "this link is no longer active, please call the agency" with the agency's number. Triggers that revoke without a person clicking: consent withdrawal (M39 outbox), `patient_caregiver` end date, discharge for a reason other than death (default: revoke at discharge plus a configurable grace of 0 days, **DECISION NEEDED clinical lead**), a staff-set block, expiry.
13. **D13: retention and legal hold.** Family-thread messages are `care_related` (7 years from `message.created_at` per ADR-0086 D2; the spec text says "6+ yr", the built period wins and the discrepancy is not touched here). They sit under the ADR-0086 hold overlay and the ADR-0055 redaction path unchanged: a message under an active legal hold is never destroyed, and a redacted message is hidden from the contact. The link principal rows (invitation, session, read trail) are access records, kept with the audit class; the token and code hashes are destroyable on revoke or expiry because they confer nothing after it. **Whether a family message is part of the designated record set (and so reachable by the patient or representative via access/amendment requests) is a DECISION NEEDED (privacy officer + counsel)**; the default is that it is, because it is retained as care-related communication.
14. **D14: not an emergency path, stated everywhere.** The link page, the auto-reply, the invitation screen and the first message the contact sends all state: this is not for emergencies; call the agency's 24-hour number (shown) or 911. The agency's number is a tenant setting that must be present or creating an invitation is refused. Messages sent after hours receive the after-hours auto-reply with the on-call number (the existing after-hours routing of WP 4.13, if enabled, is **not** used to page staff from a family message in this ADR; coupling the two is an open question).
15. **D15: behaviour after death and for bereavement.** On `patient.status = deceased` the contact's access is **not** revoked automatically for contacts that are named in the M16 bereavement plan and whose consent covers it: their principal moves to a *bereavement mode* for the plan's 13-month window (the display and programme window; retention is unchanged, D13). In bereavement mode: no new clinical content is shown; the existing thread remains readable by the contact; the staff side becomes the bereavement counsellor lane; the fixed SMS/email wording is replaced by a bereavement-appropriate neutral string (same constraints as D7; wording is for the bereavement coordinator, **not engineering**). Contacts not in the plan are revoked at the death event. At plan close the principal is revoked and the thread is archived, read-only for staff, never destroyed early. Consent in this state is the `personal_representative_authority_post_death` or the bereaved-family consent in M39; if neither is present for a contact, access ends at death. A reply after the window, to a revoked link, gets the neutral 404 page with the bereavement support number from the tenant settings.
16. **D16: audit.** Every state change (create, redeem, verify success and each failed attempt, activate, reissue, revoke, block, close, bereavement-mode switch) and every contact read and send writes an `audit_event` row in the transaction, fail closed (no audit, no response), with ids and counts only, never message text, link or code. The same PHI-audit trigger pattern as ADR-0096 D7; the contact read trail records counts only. Trail-only outbox events (`m36.family_contact.created|revoked|bereavement_mode`, ids only) are added to the catalogue with the M36 `mN.` convention. Staff reading a family thread are audited as for any patient-linked read.
17. **D17: SMS/email provider.** No transport exists. The provider (SMS, email, or both) and its BAA are a **DECISION NEEDED (counsel + privacy officer + operations)** before the first live send, and a consumer-messaging review (TCPA consent for SMS to personal numbers, STOP/HELP handling, carrier registration for a healthcare sender) belongs to counsel. The build default is *no automatic send at all in the first slices*: the staff screen shows the link and the care team delivers it by phone or copies it themselves (exactly the ADR-0096 D9 posture). Automatic sending is a later slice that cannot start until a BAA reference is recorded against the tenant, and it is bound to the fixed string only.
18. **D18: flag.** Tenant flag `messaging.family_messaging` (absent = off), **dependent** on the global trusted-proxy gate. Off: creating an invitation and every contact route answer the uniform 404; list, revoke and block do not depend on the flag. A second operational switch for automatic provider sending, `messaging.family_messaging.send`, is NOT created here; it is named only so no slice mixes the two.

## Threat model

| Threat | Control |
|---|---|
| Link forwarded or prefetched by a scanner | Single use, plus the code spoken separately (limit: the link can be consumed by a scanner; staff revoke and reissue). |
| Wrong person receives the link (misdialled number, shared phone or mailbox) | The code is the second factor; the thread shows no PHI until it is proven; staff confirm identity by call before giving the code. |
| Impersonation: a non-consented relative, an estranged person | Consent and involvement live in D2; staff create the invitation, a contact cannot self-register; a block exists. |
| Cross-patient / cross-tenant read | The thread comes from the invitation row; FORCE RLS; composite FKs; no id from the request. |
| Contact reads staff content | Contact reader is scoped to `family`-kind conversations; staff `care_team` and `bereavement` are not reachable by construction and by a live test. |
| Content in SMS/email | Fixed no-argument string, neutral sender, templates reviewed. |
| Spam, harassment, flooding | D8 budgets, close and block; no auto-delete. |
| Stale authority after revocation or death | D12 same-statement eligibility; D15 explicit rule. |
| Part 2 leak through a reply | D10 reply check; no thread for restricted patients. |
| Staff say too much to a non-representative | Composer banner; promotion to chart is explicit; clinical lead owns the "what may be said" guidance. |
| Offline cracking of code hash | Pepper outside the table (D3); attempt cap is not enough against a stolen row (stated limit). |

## Alternatives rejected

- **Reuse the M31 portal only and drop "no app".** Safest, but fails the families the item is for. The portal remains the preferred path (D1).
- **Make the contact an M31 portal account with a "lite" session.** Weakens the portal contract (password, 2FA, grants) and entangles two lifecycles; rejected for the same reason ADR-0096 rejected a `user` row for a partner.
- **Write into `care_team` / `bereavement` directly as `portal_caregiver`.** Shows staff-only history to the contact and puts a non-authenticated author into the clinical channel; rejected (D6). The M36 spec reading survives only for the portal-authenticated caregiver.
- **Plain SMS two-way with the family's own phone number.** Puts PHI on carrier networks, binds identity to a phone number that can be reassigned, and creates a record outside the cipher. Rejected.
- **Email-only magic link with no second factor.** A forwarded or misaddressed email would open a thread about a dying person; rejected.
- **Inbound-only tip box.** Fails the purpose; rejected (D5).
- **Real-time chat with presence.** Promises availability the care team cannot keep; rejected.
- **Auto-translate contact messages.** Machine translation of clinical-adjacent text is off by default (WP 6.4); rejected here.

## Known limits

- (a) A link can be consumed by a prefetcher before the person opens it; the code still protects the thread.
- (b) The code is spoken and 8 digits; a leaked hash is brute-forceable offline (ADR-0096 limit l). A pepper reduces this and does not remove it.
- (c) The contact's device and address are not bound to the session.
- (d) Content the contact types is free text and is not filtered; it may contain clinical information, other people's information or an emergency report that nobody reads in time (D14 mitigates, does not remove).
- (e) A staff member can still say too much in a reply to a person who is permitted only under 164.510(b); the product warns, it does not prevent.
- (f) Whether the portal-messaging path (M31/M36 `portal_caregiver`) is complete was not verified; if it is not, this ADR's order of preference in D1 changes.
- (g) All numbers (limits, lifetimes, lengths) are defaults, not agreed values.
- (h) Without an automatic sender the invitation is as safe as the staff member who delivers it.
- (i) Bereavement mode depends on the M16 plan being open and accurate; a missing plan means access ends at death.

## Open questions (owner)

1. **Privacy officer:** may a family member who is not a personal representative message the care team through a bearer link at all? What is the minimum identity proof for the first activation (a live call, a knowledge check)? Is a family message part of the designated record set (D13)?
2. **Privacy officer + counsel:** which relationships qualify as personal representative in each operating state; whether 164.510(b) permits staff replies to the permitted-but-not-representative contact, and with what limits; Part 2 consent mechanics for a reply (D10).
3. **Counsel:** BAA and vendor choice for SMS and email; TCPA, STOP/HELP, carrier registration; whether the neutral sender name is sufficient; state two-party/recording rules are not relevant here but state minor and guardian rules may be.
4. **Clinical lead:** what staff may say in a family thread; which roles hold `messaging.family.manage` and `.respond`; discharge-for-other-reasons behaviour; whether an unanswered family message should ever escalate.
5. **Bereavement coordinator:** `family` thread versus joining the `bereavement` channel (D6); bereavement-mode wording; whether contacts may continue after the 13 months by request.
6. **Security:** code length and pepper (D3); trusted-proxy requirement in every deployment (ADR-0096 D17); abuse budgets; whether to share session primitives with ADR-0096 or copy them.
7. **Operations:** the agency 24-hour and bereavement support numbers as required tenant settings; who watches the family lane after hours.
8. **Product owner:** the portal-first versus link-first order (D1).

## Implementation status

The build follows the slices above with these mechanics, chosen where the text left a free hand. None of them changes a decision.

- **Migrations 1230 (enum values: `channel_kind` gains `family`, `message_author_kind` gains `family_contact`) and 1231 (tables, guards, RLS, audit, permissions).** A new enum value cannot be used in the statement batch that adds it, so the two live in separate files.
- **One `family` channel per (patient, contact), subject = the patient, no `channel_member` rows at all.** The channel's visibility to staff is the care-team predicate and nothing else (the CURRENT `CareTeamProvider` roster plus the M39 Part 2 gate, the rule `effectivelyVisibleChannels` already applies to `care_team`), so the existing promotion and redaction routes work unchanged and the contact is never a member. The conversation is written through exactly two doors (`insertEncryptedMessage` with `familyThread: "contact" | "staff"`); every other writer is refused for that kind, and the flag is refused for every other kind.
- **A reissue is a new invitation row** that supersedes the live one (the old link and its sessions die in the same transaction), so a row's attempt counter never resets, as in ADR-0096 D14.
- **Eligibility (D2, D10, D12, D15) is one SQL expression** embedded in the statement that resolves the bearer and in the staff screens that show the reason, so what staff are told and what the contact may do cannot disagree. Part 2 default (D10): a patient with the `sud` privacy flag, or whose privacy-flag snapshot is being recomputed (invalidated), has no family thread; this build has no Part 2 release that names a family contact.
- **Block is lifted only by a second person**, enforced by the contact guard trigger (D8). A block revokes the live invitation in the same transaction.
- **The dead-link page shows the agency number from the URL fragment** the staff screen builds (`#...&n=`), because an unauthenticated lookup by organisation would be an existence oracle; the live number comes from the state route once the link is redeemed.
- **The code pepper is `MESSAGING_FAMILY_CODE_PEPPER` (at least 32 characters)**; without it, as without a validated `TRUSTED_PROXY_CIDRS`, the contact routes are not mounted and creating an invitation answers 503.
- **Tenant settings** (`message_family_setting`): the neutral agency name (D7), the 24-hour number (D14, required before an invitation) and the bereavement support number (D15).
