# ADR-0085 — Audited time-limited thread read for an escalated assignee who cannot view the channel

- **Status:** **Proposed** (2026-10-19). **Built so far (slice G1 only, unwired):** the grant table `message_thread_access_grant` (migration 1135) with its insert/revoke-only guard, FORCEd RLS, PHI audit trigger, the code-side constants and the `active-grant` helper. Nothing writes or reads it: slice G2 (issuing grants on escalation, the read route, revoke on terminal state or reassign) is NOT built, the tenant flag `messaging.thread_access_grant` does NOT exist (absent = off), and no web surface exists. The interim skip of ADR-0070 limit (i) is therefore still the only behaviour. It specifies the "additional mechanism" the product owner chose for decision-sheet row E5 on 2026-10-19 ("keep the built interim AND add audited, time-limited read access to that one thread"). **Every default below (the grant shape, the duration, the trigger, who grants, the audit shape, the route) is a PROPOSED engineering default of the build team, changeable by the product owner; none is a decision of a clinical lead, compliance or security. Compliance and security concurrence (E5 owners CL + COMP + SEC) is NOT recorded, and until it is, this is not built and the interim skip of ADR-0070 limit (i) is the only behaviour.** If accepted it is built flag-off and fail-closed behind a new tenant flag `messaging.thread_access_grant` (absent = off).

## Context

Checked in the code, not assumed:

- **Channel visibility is one predicate, used widely.** `canViewChannelFor` (`apps/backend/src/modules/36-messaging/messaging-route-service-db.ts`, defined around line 805) answers membership and the Part 2 gate, and is handed to services as `canViewChannel`. `canViewChannelFor` itself is named in 6 places, but the `canViewChannel` dependency it feeds appears in 134 non-test lines under `apps/backend/src` (a grep count, 2026-10-19), including `ack-service.ts`, `ack-supervisor.ts`, `unacknowledged-service.ts`, `urgent-service.ts`, `escalation-service.ts` and the notification consumers; 40 or more call sites depend on its meaning. Changing what it returns would silently widen every one of them.
- **A request is invisible to anyone who cannot view its channel.** `visibleRequest` (`ack-requests/ack-service.ts`, around line 243) reads the request, then answers one identical 404 unless `deps.canViewChannel(actor.orgId, actor.userId, row.channelId)`. The acknowledge, resolve, reassign and read routes (`routes/ack-routes.ts`) go through it.
- **The escalation walk skips a next-tier user who cannot view the channel.** `createTierDecider` (`ack-escalation/escalation-service.ts`, around line 114) requires `stillQualified` and `canViewChannel` for each candidate (ADR-0070 limit (i), the interim for E5). An after-hours on-call who is off the care team is skipped, and the ladder can end in `ladder_exhausted`.
- **The notification consumer also refuses a non-viewer.** `outbox-consumers/ack-escalation-notifications.ts` skips an assignee who cannot view the channel (`recipient_cannot_view_channel`, around line 95).
- **The first urgent send requires care-team membership and channel visibility.** In `urgent/urgent-service.ts`, `qualifiedFor` (around line 133) is `canViewChannel` AND `isQualified` (the Part 2 gate); the recipient check (around lines 181 to 188) additionally requires the recipient to be in `careTeamMembers`, else the uniform `urgentRecipientInvalid`. The sender's own channel visibility is checked at around line 144. ADR-0068 decision 6.
- **A supervisor cannot read the thread.** ADR-0078 decision 7: no read access is granted; every route that reads a request, a thread or a patient answers 404 without channel visibility.
- **A thread is a root message and its replies.** `messages.parent_message_id` (`packages/db/src/schema/messaging/messages.ts`, around line 53, a composite same-channel FK) links a reply to its root in the same channel.
- **M3 break-glass exists and is a different instrument.** `break_glass_event` (`packages/db/src/schema/break-glass.ts`) is time-boxed with `expires_at` (default +4h, D1), is actor-and-cosign accountable, and is for emergency access outside ABAC scope, not for routine routing.

## Decision (proposed)

1. **Scope: one thread, never the channel.** A grant lets one user read **one thread**: the root message plus its replies (rows of that channel whose `id` is the root, or whose `parent_message_id` is the root). It gives no channel listing, no other thread, no member list, no search, no attachments by default (open question Q3), no patient chart. The channel stays invisible to the grantee: they get no membership and appear in no member list.
2. **Never through `canViewChannelFor`.** `canViewChannelFor` and the `canViewChannel` dependency are NOT changed; none of their 40 or more call sites changes meaning. The grant is a separate, narrower check used at the explicit wiring points below, so an unaudited widening of every channel read is impossible by construction.
3. **Table `message_thread_access_grant`** (M36-owned; manifest `.ts` and `.cjs`; `arch:tenancy` three-column rule): `id`, `organization_id`, `channel_id`, `root_message_id`, `request_id`, `grantee_user_id`, `reason` (enum `escalated` \| `reassigned` \| `oncall_role_send`), `granted_at`, `expires_at`, `revoked_at` NULL. FORCEd RLS on `organization_id`. A PHI audit trigger in the style of `audit_phi_write_message_acknowledgment_request` (migration 1107), scrubbing the patient column. An **insert/revoke-only guard** (BEFORE UPDATE/DELETE): the only permitted update sets `revoked_at` from NULL to a value; no other column changes; no delete. One live grant per (`request_id`, `grantee_user_id`) (partial unique index where `revoked_at IS NULL`). **G1 hardening (migration 1135):** the insert guard requires `channel_id` to be the request's channel and `root_message_id` to be the root of the request's message thread (the message itself when it has no parent, else its parent chain followed to the top, at most 10 hops), so G2 derives channel and root server-side from the request row, never from the client; `granted_at` and `revoked_at` are forced to the database clock; `expires_at` is capped at `granted_at` + 7 days, and the per-tenant hours setting must stay under that cap; an expired but unrevoked grant still occupies the one-live slot, so G2 must revoke an expired grant before re-granting (or do both in one statement); deletion is by privilege (no DELETE grant for the app role, the destruction role only), with no delete trigger.
4. **Expiry is on the database clock.** `expires_at` is set from `now()` plus the tenant duration inside the grant's insert, and every check compares to `now()` in SQL, never to the application clock. An expired or revoked grant behaves as absent.
5. **Duration is a per-tenant setting, proposed default 4 hours.** The 4 hours is a placeholder that mirrors the M3 break-glass default of +4h (`break-glass.ts`); **it is not a clinical position and no clinical lead has set it.** The setting is `thread_access_grant_hours`; where it is stored (an existing messaging tenant-settings mechanism or a new column) is chosen in slice G1 and was not found in the code reading. Bounds (minimum and maximum) are open (Q2).
6. **Who grants, and when (proposed): the system, as part of the same transaction that makes the person the assignee.** (a) `escalated`: the escalation write (`writeEscalation` in `escalation-service.ts`) creates the grant when the tier's chosen user qualifies (`stillQualified`, including the Part 2 gate) but cannot view the channel. (b) `reassigned`: the supervisor or participant reassign creates it for the new assignee in the same situation. (c) `oncall_role_send`: the after-hours role send of ADR-0082 creates it for the confirmed on-call when they qualify but are off the care team or outside the channel. No human grants it ad hoc in v1 (Q4).
7. **Revoke on terminal state or reassign.** The grant is revoked (`revoked_at`) in the transaction that moves the request to `resolved` or `cancelled`, and in the transaction that reassigns or escalates it away from the grantee. `failed_unreachable` is not terminal (it is in `ACK_OPEN_STATUSES`), so it does not revoke by itself; expiry still applies.
8. **What a grantee can do.** (a) `GET` the request, (b) acknowledge it, (c) resolve it after acknowledging (the existing routes in `ack-routes.ts`, through a grant-aware `visibleRequest`), and (d) read that one thread (new route, decision 9). **No composer**: a grantee cannot post a message or reply, and **no attachments by default** (Q3). A grantee who wants to write replies in the thread, or add context, is out of scope and open (Q5).
9. **New read route:** `GET /api/v1/messaging/escalations/:id/thread` (`:id` is the request id). It requires `messaging.read`, an unexpired, unrevoked grant for the caller on that request, and the Part 2 gate; anything else is the same uniform 404. It is audited with `resourceIdParam: "id"` (the repo rule that a read-audit config needs a `resourceIdParam`, so the read event carries the resource id), and the live suite asserts that the read event lands. Each read is an audit event; the grant, each read and the expiry or revoke are the audit shape (Q6).
10. **Never bypasses the Part 2 gate.** A grant is created only for a user for whom `isQualified` / `stillQualified` (the M39-consent-backed Part 2 gate in `part2-channel-gate.ts`) answers true for the channel's patient, and every grant-aware check re-asks it at read time. A restricted (Part 2) channel whose gate refuses the grantee yields no grant and a uniform 404. Withdrawn consent, which the existing gate and `m39-consent-revoked.ts` consumer already honour, ends the read.
11. **Wiring points (all behind the flag; each is an addition, not a change to `canViewChannelFor`).**
    - `ack-requests/ack-service.ts` `visibleRequest` (around line 243): admit a caller who holds a live grant for that request (get, acknowledge, resolve). Other `canViewChannel` checks in the file (around lines 341 and 456) stay.
    - `ack-escalation/escalation-service.ts` `createTierDecider` (around line 114): a candidate who qualifies but cannot view the channel is still routed when the flag is on, and `writeEscalation` creates the grant in the same transaction. Flag off: the interim skip is unchanged.
    - `urgent/urgent-service.ts` (care-team and channel checks around lines 133 to 144 and 181 to 188): only for the `oncall_role_send` path, the care-team and channel-visibility requirement is replaced by the grant for the confirmed on-call; `isQualified` is kept. The ordinary person-send keeps ADR-0068 decision 6.
    - Notification consumers (`outbox-consumers/ack-escalation-notifications.ts`, `ack-notifications.ts`, `urgent-notifications.ts`): treat a live grant as "can view" for that request, so the assignee is alerted rather than skipped.
    - The new read route of decision 9.
12. **Flag, permission, fail-closed.** Flag `messaging.thread_access_grant` (absent = off); off is the uniform 404 and the interim skip. No new permission is proposed for a grantee: the grant is the authority, on top of `messaging.read` and the existing urgent-receive keys the user already needs to be qualified. If the grant check or the duration setting cannot be read, the answer is "no grant" (deny), never "allow".

## What this amends

Only if this ADR is accepted; both amendments are scoped and flag-gated.

- **ADR-0078 decision 7** says: "No read access is granted: every route that reads a request, a thread or a patient still answers 404 without channel visibility." **Changes:** with this ADR, that sentence gains one exception: a user holding a live `message_thread_access_grant` for a request may get, acknowledge and resolve that request and read that one thread through `GET /api/v1/messaging/escalations/:id/thread`. **Unchanged:** a supervisor (`messaging.review`) still cannot read the thread, acknowledge, resolve or cancel; a grant is never created for a supervisor because they are a supervisor; every other route still answers 404 without channel visibility; the explicit-acknowledgment rule (ADR-0056 / ADR-0069) is untouched.
- **ADR-0070 accepted limit (i)** says: the walk does not choose a next-tier user who cannot view the request's channel (interim built). **Changes:** with the flag on, the walk chooses a qualified next-tier user who cannot view the channel and grants them the thread; with the flag off, or when the user is not qualified, or the Part 2 gate refuses, the interim skip and `ladder_exhausted` behaviour stand exactly as built. **Unchanged:** the E5 interim as the default; `makeStillQualified`; the first-send rules; the supervisor alert at the end of the ladder.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Make `canViewChannelFor` grant-aware.** It has 40 or more call sites; any grant would widen channel lists, search, attachments and every other read at once, with no audit. The narrow, separate check is the point of decision 2.
- **Add the assignee to the channel (a membership row).** Reveals the whole channel and the member list, persists after the need ends, and is not time-limited.
- **Reuse M3 `break_glass_event`.** It is an emergency override of ABAC with cosign semantics, invoked by the actor; routine after-hours routing would normalise break-glass and flood its review queue. **Offered as an alternative for compliance review:** if compliance prefers one access-exception register, the grant could be expressed as a system-issued break-glass event with a +4h expiry, at the cost of the problems above.
- **Require care-team membership for the on-call.** Skips the after-hours on-call, which is the practical gap (ADR-0070 limit (i), Hazard B of ADR-0082).
- **Give the supervisor thread read.** Not requested; ADR-0078 decision 7 keeps the supervisor out of the thread.
- **A grantee composer.** Turns a read exception into a write path into a patient thread with a person who is not on the care team.

## Plan of engineering slices (flag-off, once accepted and concurrence is recorded)

- **G1, grant schema.** The table, guards, audit trigger, manifest entries, the tenant duration setting, the flag key; live suite for RLS as the real `wardenx_app` role, the insert/revoke-only guard and expiry on the database clock. Independent of the other slices.
- **G2, grant wiring.** The wiring points of decision 11 and the read route of decision 9, with the live suite that the read event lands, that a restricted channel yields a 404, and that flag off leaves the interim skip. **Edits `urgent/urgent-service.ts` and `oncall-directory/role-send-service.ts`, as do slices S3 and L2 of ADR-0082, so these three are serialized and never run in parallel.** Depends on G1.
- **G3, web.** A read-only thread view for a grantee with the expiry shown as text, and no composer. Depends on G2.

## Consequences (if accepted)

- One table, one setting, one flag, one read route and five small wiring changes; no change to `canViewChannelFor`.
- The after-hours on-call can be escalated to, alerted, and can acknowledge, which closes the practical gap of ADR-0082 hazard B; they see one thread for a limited time and every read is audited.

## Known ceilings

- A grant widens who can see PHI for the grant's lifetime; its safety rests on the expiry, the revoke rules, the Part 2 re-check at read and the audit, none of which is yet reviewed by compliance or security.
- The duration is a placeholder.
- Expiry cuts a read; it does not recall what a user already saw.
- Where the duration setting lives was not found in the code reading and is chosen in G1.
