# WARDENX_UI_PATIENT_FAMILY_PORTAL_DASHBOARD

Version: 1.0  
Status: Screen Specification  
Module: Patient / Family Portal

---

## Purpose

The portal dashboard gives patients and authorized family members a clear, simple view of care team, upcoming visits, messages, documents, and support options.

---

## Primary Users

- Patient
- Family caregiver
- Authorized representative

---

## Layout Pattern

Use Portal Dashboard.

```text
Portal Header
Patient Summary
Care Team
Upcoming Visits
Messages
Documents / Consents
Help
```

---

## Key Cards

- Care Team
- Next Visit
- Messages
- Documents to Review
- Medications if enabled
- Emergency / after-hours contact

---

## Tone

Portal language must be plain, compassionate, and non-technical.

---

## Actions

- Message care team
- View schedule
- Open document
- Join telehealth
- Update contact info
- View patient rights
