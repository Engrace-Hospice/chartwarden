# WARDENX_UI_BEREAVEMENT

Version: 1.0  
Status: Screen Specification  
Module: Bereavement

---

## Purpose

The Bereavement screen manages post-death bereavement follow-up, risk scoring, contact schedules, counseling notes, and 13-month follow-up.

---

## Primary Users

- Bereavement Counselor
- Social Worker
- Chaplain
- Administrator
- Compliance Officer

---

## Layout Pattern

Use Work Queue + Patient/Family Detail.

```text
Page Header
KPI Strip
Bereavement Queue
Right: Family / Contact Plan Detail
```

---

## KPI Cards

- Active Bereavement Plans
- High Risk Families
- Contacts Due
- Overdue Contacts
- Completed This Month

---

## Queue Columns

- Deceased patient
- Primary bereaved contact
- Relationship
- Risk level
- Next contact due
- Plan status
- Last contact
- Owner

---

## Bereavement Detail

Sections:

- Deceased patient summary
- Bereaved contacts
- Risk assessment
- Follow-up schedule
- Contact history
- Counseling notes
- Referrals
- Closure summary

---

## Risk Levels

- Low
- Moderate
- High
- Complicated grief risk

---

## Actions

- Create bereavement plan
- Add contact
- Complete contact
- Update risk score
- Refer to counseling
- Close plan

---

## Timeline

Must show 13-month follow-up schedule and completed contacts.
