# WARDENX_UI_DENIALS_MANAGEMENT

Version: 1.0  
Status: Screen Specification  
Module: Denials Management

---

## Purpose

Denials Management tracks denied claims, denial reasons, appeals, corrective actions, and trends.

---

## Primary Users

- Biller
- Billing Manager
- Administrator
- Compliance Officer

---

## Layout Pattern

Use Work Queue.

```text
Page Header
KPI Strip
Denials Queue
Right: Denial Detail / Appeal Actions
```

---

## KPI Cards

- Open Denials
- Appeal Due
- Denials This Month
- Dollars at Risk
- Overturned Appeals

---

## Queue Columns

- Claim
- Patient
- Payer
- Denial reason
- Amount
- Appeal deadline
- Status
- Owner
- Actions

---

## Detail Panel

Sections:

- Claim summary
- Denial reason
- CARC/RARC
- Supporting documentation
- Appeal timeline
- Corrective action
- Related claims

---

## Statuses

- New
- Under Review
- Appeal Draft
- Submitted
- Overturned
- Upheld
- Written Off
- Closed

---

## Actions

- Assign owner
- Draft appeal
- Attach evidence
- Submit appeal
- Mark resolved
- Create prevention action
